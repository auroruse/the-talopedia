**Flandonia** or officaly known as the Kingdom of Flandonia is a nation situated in western [[evria]], it has a coast line on the North Sea and shares its borders with [[alemannia]] and [[portal-arverne|Arverne]]. The Country of Flandonia is divided into 12 different provinces, and Has two Overseas Territories, Karbodia and Southern Ödland. Its biggest City is Spitdaam, located in the province of Algen, With its capital, Dortdaam being the second largest in the Nation. For its overseas colonies, Neiuw-Zije in Southern Ödland and Dunnborg, commonly called by the locals as "Sai-Gau".

## Etymology

The Name Flandonia is a very recent term for Flandonia, it was originally called Flandrig, Fland meaning the old capital/people who inhabited the land, and Rig meaning "People of"  this was the name of the country while in the Holy Evrian Empire. After 1815, the nation wanted to show its independence by changing its name, And after a few debates over is the name should be Flania or Flandonia, Flandonia was eventually picked on January 5th, 1816. This day is commonly known as "Name Day" in Flandonia.

## History

The earliest known human activity goes back to 600,000 (599,000 BCE) years ago with the oldest fossil being found in Ranbelt. (A small village outside of Welesburg) Other Acient Traces can be found near Spanje, including Alder, a acient settlement found in 1878. These people are known as the Qatians

The Qatians came from the south, specifically Barbary. And migrated North, finally settling in Sepjik Vally. They moved more north around 500,000 Years ago, and inhabited the other areas of what is now modern day Flandonia.

In 1 AD, the Rumaic Empire Invaded. And they named the area Qatia, After the fall of the Rumaic Empire around 400 AD, the Nation gained its independence as a small group of City-States at this time it was named "Flandrig" after the the Capital of the City-States, "Fland" and the Modern name of the Qatians, Flandorians (Also coming from the name of the City) around 690 AD, The Kingdom of Hougmont, and Flandrig Merged. Due to Prince Frithoric III of Flandrig and Princess Ida I of Hougmont marrying. After this union, Flandrig became known as the Kingdom of Flanmont.

The nation was then invaded by Akenauer Empire in 851 AD (Modern [[alemannia]]) the Akenauer Empire Changed Flanmonts name back to Flandrig. They were also turned into a duchy. By the time of the Holy Evrian Empire in 1040, they were still attached to Akenauer.

Flandrigian forces fought during the Čevarene Wars (1418-1432) and the Twenty Five Years' Wars (1616–1641) And other various wars and conflicts throughout the 1700s.

In 1805, the Holy Evrian Empire was dissolved, and Flandrig Gained independence, First as the "Holy Republic of Flandonia" and then the "Kingdom of Flandonia".

In 1828, A Civil War sweeped through the nation. Fought between The "Prinsgezindists" and the "Nationalists", After 2 years of fighting, the Prinsgezindists (Royalists) won. The first king since the Flandmont era was crowned on March 20th, 1831.

In 1857, after a short War with the Empire of Bang-Lah, the area of the former empire was made the "Crown Colony of Karbodia" Named after a mispronounceianion of the word "Karboa" a local name for the region. Soon after, the East Karbodia Flander Company was founded, and shortly after that, the colony of Southern Ödland was founded in 1884 during the Scramble for Pelagonia.

In 1906 Flandonia joined the Central Powers due to its strong ties with the Holy Evrian Empire, when fighting began in 1914, they joined on the Southern front. By 1918 Flandonia had suffered major losses to both people and infrastructure. Currently, flandonia is dealing with issues that aroused during the Great War, mainly Colonial.
