**Daedalus** (sometimes stylized as **DAEDALUS**) is a global clandestine paramilitary organization and secret society, currently headquartered in [[axiom]], [[elysian-state-of-unity|E.S.U]]. Named after the mythological Astriyan craftsman and architect, Daedalus is a decades-old entity with the aim of global governance and "perfection of human nature".

Born from Enlightenment-era ideas, Daedalus was originally founded in [[alemannia|Central Evria]] in June of 1776 by lawyer and writer Johann Adam Knigge, with the primary goals of advancing their ideas of political and societal equality and justice, as well as opposing religious control over public life, superstition, and abuse of power by monarchies, which were prevalent over [[evria]], and, by extension, the world as a whole. The group grew in popularity, attracting many members from the intelligentsia and literati of the day, until the group and its derivatives were banned by decree of the Holy Evrian Emperor Maximilian III in 1786. The group promptly went underground, continuing to operate in secret. During this tumultuous time, the group's ideas underwent a radical shift toward global governance, seeing the separation and injustice carried out by the monarchs of the world as a hindrance to their primary goals.

The organization would continue garnering members from all walks of life, accruing a decent chunk of wealth and influence within many corporations and governments. The organization's breakthrough would come at the beginning of the 20th century with the emergence of [[archer-maine]]'s ideas of [[gestaltism]]. His views and ideas were a perfect fit for the plans they had for the world, leading the organization to sponsor his efforts massively, eventually ending up with Maine as a core member of the organization and the birth of the E.S.U.

The organization is ruled by an exclusive circle of people known only as the Wisemen's Council, made up of 12 representatives of varying backgrounds. While their goals are benevolent, the organization isn't above using violence and intimidation to achieve its goals.

## Etymology

The organization's name was chosen to be the same as [the character](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daedalus) from ancient Astriyan mythology. Adam Knigge chose the name on the grounds of what he interpreted the character to stand for. Knigge understood Daedalus to be aiming for the same goals as he and his organization were: talented or influential people giving those with power what they desire most, only to eventually use the same people to achieve a greater goal of their own. Despite being the father of Icarus, he was the one to warn him against flying too close to the sun.

While the organization's name was chosen mostly for symbolic reasons, its usage to refer to a singular individual has often been used to maintain the organization's secrecy, often used to shrug off any accusations or leaks onto singular individuals.

## History

### Albernian Circle

While similar ideas shared by other Enlightenment-era thinkers were held by people centuries before them, it was only during the 1700's that these ideas of equality and justice became more widespread, and in the case of some regions, mainstream. During his time at Ludovicus University studying law, Adam Knigge became well acquainted with the finer points of these ideas, despite having been influenced by Enlightenment and Rationalist schools of thought through his uncle, Christian Knigge, a professor of civil law.

Following Knigge's graduation with a doctorate in law, he would be inducted into his then hometown's local Masonic Lodge in Falkenberg by a set of close associates. He would quickly network with various members of his Grand Lodge, eventually rising to the rank of Master Mason. He would come into contact with multiple other students and experts in law, with whom he'd lay the groundwork for what would later become Daedalus, a group referred to only as the Albernian Circle (*Albenianischer Kreis*).

While he and his associates would attempt to push his ideas to a wider group of Freemasons, they would their ideas often rejected by more senior members of the organization. The trend of rejection, including the mounting costs of participation in Masonic ritual, would lead Knigge and his associates to officially establish the Circle as a separate entity from the Freemasons, which he would eventually name after the character of Astriyan mythology.

### Exile and Rise to Power (1786-1864)

Following a few years of succesful operation and networking, the organization would face a major crisis after then Holy Evrian Emperor, Kaiser Maximilian III, would announce a comprehensive and total outlawing of the Albernian Circle. This decision came some time after rumours began spreading of the group's anti-monarchical ideas, as well as an apparent lobby by the Freemasons accusing the Circle of spreading atheistic thinking. This would eventually lead to the arrest of a fair few of the Circle's key members, as well as a slew of other innocent people only claimed to have been working with the Circle. Knigge and the remaining members would flee into exile, with many ending up in Elvester and the United States of Elysia, a nation which government had many Freemasons in positions of power.

Daedalus, which from this point on exclusively went by that name, would go completely underground, disappearing from any public records, and as far as mainstream history was concerned: dissolved in 1786. Knigge would do what he could to aid his former members from exile, writing multiple pamphlets and books to attempt to aid in their trials, however would prove mostly unsuccesful. During his time in Elysia, Knigge would eventually come into contact with Vergilius Mountbatten, managing to induct him and a slew of other founders of the United States into Daedalus. With this first step, Daedalus would slowly begin creeping its way into various other global institutions, gathering many globally important politicians, businessmen, and scientists within the organisation's ranks. It was also common for Daedalus to poach members away from local Masonic lodges.

It was during this time that Daedalus would oversee an ideological shift. While Knigge would remain as head of the organization for decades after its founding, he would eventually step down from his position due to his age and deteriorating health in 1827. He would die three years later at the age of 82, leaving behind a manifesto that dictated the future of his organization. He argued that the primary blocks against the mission of Daedalus weren't the clergy and monarchy, but national borders in general. He argued that a true state of reason, of illumination, could only be achieved with the elimination of national borders across the globe. This manifesto would form the central pillar of all of Daedalus' philosophical literature and thinking, remaining the only such work up until the 1920's.

### Prelude to Revolution (1863 - 1918)

Beginning in the later half of the 19th century, Daedalus and its associated groups and corporations began heavily investing in the creation of an armed wing of the organization to be used directly when application of armed force was required. This project would eventually result in the creation of the Talons Division, an elite group of assassins and spies trained to eliminate specific targets who the organization deems undesirable or outright dangerous to their agenda or operations.

Additionally, Daedalus would cooperate with the government of the United States of Elysia on a special project starting in the late 1890's, dubbed Project Prometheus. Operating through a series of blacksites and unmapped laboratories, Prometheus aimed to create a successor to the Talons Division, an operative raised and molded to obey the organization or its proxies unquestionably, while possessing the abilities and training comparable to the best of the best of the Talons Division. Personnel assigned to the project would adopt young children from orphanages, or in more extreme cases, kidnap homeless children to use as test subjects for the project. The overwhelming use of conditioning techniques combined with varying cocktails of drugs would take its toll on the vast majority of test subjects, with only two exceptions emerging at the tail end of the project's lifespan: a boy only known as Michael, and [[cassandra-conway]], who would be inducted into the USE's Military Intelligence Service, and later become the first and only director of [[majesty]].

Daedalus' cooperation with the USE would fall short however following the election of Vincent Stone as president of the United States. While Stone was a collaborator with Daedalus, his personal grievances and bias that radicalized following the end of the Brethren War caused him to break away Elysian institutions from the control of Daedalus and form power around himself, enabling him to exercise practically dictatorial powers over the United States. This would leave Daedalus on the backfoot in, what was for most of their history, their home turf.

### From Civil War to an Invisible War (1918 - Present)

Being left without a credible foothold within Elysia, Daedalus scrambled to find any possible holes in Stone's administration that they could exploit. This opportunity would come with the meteoric rise of both the Spartacus Division, and later the People's Volunteer Corps. While not directly supporting the movement, Daedalus would use the Volunteer Corps' march through Elysia to its advantage, taking many disenfranchised, homeless, or abandoned people into their training as field operatives. Following the end of the Civil War, Daedalus would reassert its control over the assets previously under Stone's command.

During the political power struggle of 1922 over the Reconstruction Authority, Daedalus would turn its attention towards one particular individual vying for power. The former chairman of the Communist Party of Elysia, Archer Maine. Following the publishing of his manifesto and ideological founding text, *One Choir*, Daedalus would officially make contact with the man, finding his ideas of a global borderless society to be a perfect match for the agenda they were looking to advance. They sponsored his political campaigns immensely, greatly assisting in his party's ascension to power, and by extension, the creation of the Unity State. Maine would be made a core member of the Wisemen's Council, and many of his ministers members of the organization.

The greatest crisis the organization faced after the civil war would be in 1928 when Conway, a key asset and member of Daedalus, defected along with an entire branch of the Unity State's Office of Naval Intelligence. The most worrying aspect of this for Daedalus was the fact that Conway paraded around the world under the same name as the organization she defected from, bringing the risk of Daedalus' exposure to the world to the forefront. However, Conway's own actions, as well as the leaks from former allies of hers, would eventually result in her capture and execution in Elysian custody.

## Structure

Daedalus is directed by a group of 12 people known as the Wisemen's Council. Each member is exclusively selected by the council itself, requiring at least a slight majority to enact decisions. Each council member is usually a representative of particular nations, as well as the Daedalus presence within each nation. However, each member maintains a heavy degree of anonimity, only showing their face in times of crisis when typical arrangements have to be discarded in favour of swift decisions.

While generally a secular organization, Daedalus maintains a variety of initiation rites for prospective and new members, many of which utilize symbolism and ideas from Nazarism, particularly derived from Masonic teachings.

While a largely decentralized organization, many of its organisational elements and offices are hosted within the E.S.U, particularly in Axiom's Dynamo Building, as well as the Mt. Dolor Mountain Complex located in the rural Apalchen Mountains. Both are frequent meeting places for the organization's leadership.

Espionage operations are divided between a variety of theaters, Evrian, Valtherian, Elysian or Domestic, and Nabatea. Additionally, the Talons Division operates above the oversight of every theater, bypassing the authority of theater leads as required to complete their assigned missions. Each theater, as well as the Talons Division, is overseen by a member of the organization's elite "Red Cell" list of operatives, originally created to probe Elysia's own defenses and participate in training exercises as an elite enemy force. Listed by codename, Evria is overseen by Garnet, Valtheria by Burgundy, Elysia by Silvereye, Nabatea by Scarlet, and Talons by Crimson.
