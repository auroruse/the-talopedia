**Daedalus** (sometimes stylized as **DAEDALUS**) is a global clandestine paramilitary organization and secret society, currently headquartered in Axiom, E.S.U. Named after the mythological Astriyan craftsman and architect, Daedalus is a decades-old entity with the aim of global governance and "perfection of human nature".

Born from Enlightenment-era ideas, Daedalus was originally founded in Central Evria in June of 1776 by lawyer and writer Johann Adam Knigge, with the primary goals of advancing their ideas of political and societal equality and justice, as well as opposing religious control over public life, superstition, and abuse of power by monarchies, which were prevalent over Evria, and by extension the world as a whole. The group grew in popularity, attracting many members from the intelligentsia and literati of the day, until the group and its derivatives were banned by decree of the Holy Evrian Emperor Maximilian III in 1786. The group promptly went underground, continuing to operate in secret. During this tumultous time, the group's ideas would undergo a radical shift toward global governance, seeing the separation and injustice carried out by the monarchs of the world as a hinderance toward their primary goals.

The organization would continue garnering members from all walks of life, accruing a decent chunk of wealth and influence within many corporations and governments. The organization's breakthrough would come at the beginning of the 20th century with the emergence of Archer Maine's ideas of Gestaltism. His views and ideas were a perfect fit for the plans they had for the world, leading the organization to sponsor his efforts massively, eventually ending up with Maine as a core member of the organization, and the birth of the E.S.U.

The organization is ruled by an exclusive circle of people known only as the Wisemen's Council, made up of 12 representatives of varying backgrounds. While their goals are benevolent, the organization isn't above using violence and intimidation to achieve its goals.
