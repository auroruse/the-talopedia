## *Accion Meridiana*

***Accion Meridiana*** (**AM**) is a far-right, integral nationalist political party in the **Republic of Carestur**. Originating in the mid-1920s as an extra-parliamentary nationalist league during the political and economic crises of the Third Republic, it reorganized as a formal electoral party following the promulgation of the **Constitucion de Carestur** in 1934.

In the ***Parlament de Carestur***, Accion Meridiana sits in hardline opposition to the minority **FRP-ADT** coalition government of Prime Minister **Jean-Baptiste Cazals**, holding **29 of 577 seats** in the ***Assemblada Nacionala*** and **17 of 348 seats** in the ***Conselh de la Republica*** on a national vote share of approximately **5%**.

## History

### Origins in the Interwar Leagues (1926-1934)

Accion Meridiana emerged in **Tolosa** and **Marsillia** in 1926 out of disaffected Great War veterans' associations, monarchist-traditionalist societies, and radical nationalist student circles. Capitalizing on postwar sovereign debt crises, currency instability, and widespread industrial strikes under the **Third Caresturian Republic**, the movement blamed parliamentary democracy and socialist labor syndicates for national decline. Throughout the late 1920s and early 1930s, AM's uniformed street sections frequently clashed with militant trade unions in industrial centers such as **Barcino**, and **Marsillia.**

### Opposition to the 1934 Constitutional Settlement

During the constitutional crisis of early 1934, Accion Meridiana agitated for the complete abolition of republican parliamentarism in favor of an authoritarian national state. When the ***Movement de Renovacion Meridiana*** convened the ***Convencion Constitucionala de Tolosa*** and established the semi-presidential **Fourth Republic** on **1 June 1934**, AM denounced the new charter as an artificial technocratic compromise that preserved party politics under the oversight of the ***Conselh Superior de la Competéncia***.

### Electoral Entry under the Fourth Republic (1934-1936)

Confronted with strict public-order statutes enforced by the new regime, which threatened to dissolve militant leagues operating outside the constitutional framework, Accion Meridiana formally registered as a political party to contest the founding elections of the Fourth Republic. While winning only **5%** of the national vote, its concentrated support in conservative coastal enclaves, border towns, and hardline settler constituencies secured **29 deputies** in the ***Assemblada Nacionala*** and **17 councillors** in the ***Conselh de la Republica***.

### Ideology and Platform

<figure class="float float-right" style="width:300px"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/eEP75oI.jpeg" alt="***Maurici Valette Speaking at the Tribune***"><figcaption>***Maurici Valette Speaking at the Tribune***</figcaption></figure>

**Integral Nationalism and Executive Authoritarianism:** Accion Meridiana rejects liberal parliamentarism and political pluralism, advocating a centralized, authoritarian state ordered around traditional hierarchy, military strength, and cultural unity. Its doctrinal publications routinely attack both the ***Partit Socialista de Doas Ribas*** **(PSDR)** and the moderate republicanism of the **FRP** and **ADT**.

**Economic Corporatism:** Opposing both Marxist class struggle and laissez-faire financial capitalism, AM promotes a corporatist economic model in which employers and workers are organized into state-supervised vertical syndicates, alongside high protective tariffs for Caresturian agriculture and heavy industry.

*Hardline Colonialism and Opposition to the* Acte d'Algernon: **A central pillar of AM's platform in 1936 is uncompromising opposition to the** *Acte d'Algernon* **of** 23 January 1936 **. The party condemns the transformation of** Numidia **into a self-governing** Autonomous Territory **, the establishment of a bilingual** Assemblada Territoriala, and the recognition of Arabic and Berber as co-official languages, demanding instead the restoration of direct metropolitan rule and unrestricted European settlement.

## Organization and Electoral Base

### Party Apparatus and Statuory Firewalls

Headquartered in **Tolosa**, Accion Meridiana is directed by 
**Maurici Valette**

and propagates its platform through its daily newspaper, ***L'Espasa Meridiana***, and its youth organization, the ***Joventuts Meridianas***. Under the Fourth Republic's statutory firewalls separating registered political parties from paramilitary leagues and trade unions, AM has been compelled to maintain a formal legal separation between its parliamentary caucus and its affiliated street and veterans' associations to avoid judicial dissolution.

### Geographic Support

<figure class="float float-right" style="width:300px"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/uzJ9v7o.jpeg" alt="National Headquarters of *Accion Meridiana* in Tolosa"><figcaption>National Headquarters of *Accion Meridiana* in Tolosa</figcaption></figure>

Despite its small national vote share, AM maintains distinct regional pockets of strength:

**Metropolitan Carestur:** Draws support from conservative rentiers, traditionalist professionals, and nationalist circles in resort cities such as **Marsillia**, and **Tolosa**, as well as border districts wary of external instability.

**Autonomous Numidia:** Enjoys disproportionate influence among hardline European *colon* landowners and urban settler militants in **Algernon**, where its opposition to indigenous political representation resonates strongly.
