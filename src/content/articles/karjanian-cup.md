The Karjanian Cup (Routan: *Karjanmaan Cup*) is the domestic cup competition of [[karjania]] and it's territories. The final is held annually at the Red Arena (*Punainen Areena*) in [[pietari]]. It has been held since 1897, with exceptions during [[great-war]] and the Karjanian Civil War. It is organized by the Karjanian Football Association (KFA).

## Structure

The Karjanian Cup is held over 6 rounds, and includes 48 teams, with no qualification phase. In the first round, or the round of 64, KPL teams aren't yet included in the competition. The competition includes teams from the [[karjanian-premier-league]], the [[karjanian-secondary-league]], the Karjanian Kolmonen, the Kulanmaan Cup and the Frederikka Cup. The games are single legged, and the lower division side hosts the games. In case both teams are from the same division, the game is held at the stadium of the team currently sitting lower in the table. Teams from Kulanmaan and Frederikka are considered lower division than the Kolmonen,the third and bottom division of Karjanian football. The final is held at the Red Arena in Pietari, with almost 50,000 capacity, also the home stadium of the Karjanian National Team.  In case of a draw, 30 minutes of extra time follow with no golden goal rule. If the game is still tied after, it goes to a penalty shootout, each team taking either five penalties or until one team outscores the other in the instant death format. The Karjanian Cup is a knockout competition with single elimination.

## History

The Karjanian Cup was preceded by the Karjanian Royal Shield, a single elimination knockout tournament held before the creation of a Karjanian domestic league. In 1896, the first season of the Karjanian Royal First League, the predecessor of the Karjanian Premier League was held. After this, in 1897, the KFA announced the creation of a new, knockout competition, that included every single professional team playing in Karjania. The first running was won by Pietari FK. Tsirvakka JK, who weren't members of the Karjanian domestic league system, won the 1898 edition, beating RöPS 4-1 in the final. This is still the largest winning margin in a Karjanian Cup final to this day. Kuittijärvi JK lost both the 1899 and the 1900 finals 2-1, to Roudanjärvi JK and Pietari FK respectively. Åulot FK became the second non-league team to win in 1901, when they defeated Syväsjärvi 2-1 in the final. Due to construction in the Red Arena, the 1902 final, won by Syväsjärvi JK over Murmánska Partisani was held at the Roudanjärvi National Stadium instead. Roudanjärvi JK won the 1903 final, while Red Star Roudanjärvi won the 1904 one. Kuittijärvi JK beat Musta Roudanjärvi 2-1 in the 1905 final, the first and only Karjanian Cup final to not feature a team from the domestic first division. The 1906 final between RöPS and Red Star Roudanjärvi was the first one to feature a penalty shootout, which was ultimately won by the team from Römpöla. Union Nowgorod took their first and only title after beating Rautaniemi JK in the 1907 final. The next four editions in 1908, 1909, 1910 and 1911 were won by Roudanjärvi JK, Pietari FK, RöPS and Red Star Roudanjärvi. The 1912 final brought the first victory to a team from Saamitmaa, when Murmánska Partisani beat RöPS 2-0. Kalaraava JK became the first team to go back to back, winning in both 1913 and 1914. They won the second division dominantly in 1914, but due to The Great War, they couldn't play in the first division until 1918. Most of their players were conscripted, and the 1914 Karjanian Cup was the last throphy the club ever won. They are the last team to win from outside the domestic first division. After the Great War, Pietari FK and Red Star Roudanjärvi won the 1918 and 1919 editions, the final two before the Karjanian Civil War. Unlike most football competitions, the format of the Karjanian Cup stayed the same even after the Civil War. The first running held under the USSK was won by Roudanjärvi JK 1-0 over Syväsjärvi JK in 1921. VKS wzln the 1922 title, and Syväsjärvi JK took it home in 1923. Pietari FK won it's 5th title in 1924, while Red Star Roudanjärvi won it's 4th the following edition in 1925. VKS won in 1926 and RöPS in 1927. Murmánska Partisani won both the 1928 and 1929 titles, becoming the first 1st division team to go back to back. Then, Pietari FK won three in a row between 1930 and 1932, winning their 8th title in the process, making them by far the most successful team in Karjanian Cup history. Åulot FK won the 1933 edition over Arkankeli FK 2-1, winning their 2nd title.
