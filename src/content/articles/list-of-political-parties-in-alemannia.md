This is a list of political parties in Alemannia. Fourteen parties hold seats in the Nationalrat, the lower house of the Bundesversammlung, following the general elections of 10 February 1934, the first held in the nation. All twelve besides the regional parties were founded in 1931, following the unification of the Alemannian states Ymiria, Arlbern and Nordmark into the Alemannian Confederation and the merger of the political parties of the three nations. The current ruling government of is formed by the  Democratic People's Party (DVP), the  Evrian Commonwealth Movement (ECB), the  Civil Alliance Alemannia (BAA), the  Social Democratic Workers' Party  (SDAP), the  Alemannian Farmers Party (BPA), the  Nazarite Democratic Union (NDU) and relies on the support of the Čevarene Social Democratic Party (TSPD/SDSČ). The largest opposition party is the  Alemannian Communist Party (KPA), led by Ernst Halber.
