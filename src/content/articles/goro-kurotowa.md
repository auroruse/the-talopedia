Goro Kurotowa (クロトワ五郎, born July 4, 1898) is an Elysian-born special forces and former espionage operative and mercenary commander. He served as an espionage operative for the USE’s Military Intelligence Service, the Unity State’s Office of Naval Intelligence, and later its Special Recon Service Branch No.12, also known as [[majesty]]. He was instrumental in the founding of MAJESTY as a separate entity from the Unity State, having made many deals with multiple foreign governments, and was the founder of the private military company [[soldiers-without-borders]]. With a service record stretching across multiple decades, he remains a ghost story to the wider world, his existence and defection to the Miyamoto Shogunate being masked by his supposed death.

## Early Life

Born on the 4th of July, 1898, in Port of Muscovite, United States of Elysia, Kurotowa was the eldest son of Kazuhira Kurotowa, a dockworker and Nichirian immigrant originally from the city of Makinohara, and Alice Campbell, a nurse and granddaughter of a semi-renowned Elysian Volunteer fighter in Evria during the rebellions against Napolyon II’s rule of the continent. The two would have 2 other children, one of which, his younger brother, died at age 3 from complications related to Polio.

The death of his youngest sibling caused great strife at home, with her mother entering a depressive state which would lead to her taking her own life in 1909. His wife’s death would cause Kurotowa’s father to turn to liquor, a habit which would leave the family nearly penniless were it not for Kurotowa picking up odd jobs around town to help his remaining family. When he turned 17, his father would succumb to his addiction, leaving Kurotowa to provide for his younger sister and himself. He would receive an end all be all offer from a mysterious benefactor working for the government. His sister and himself would be provided for, if he were to enlist with the Specialist Training Program in the Maplestone Military Academy.  With practically nothing else to lose, Kurotowa accepted almost immediately. He would clear the entrance examinations with high grades, eventually graduating as an espionage operative for the Elysian Military Intelligence Service in May of 1916.

## Service in MIS

Kurotowa’s service within the MIS began almost immediately after his graduation at the age of 18. He was assigned to an infiltration mission to Veynach, Albern, as part of Operation Alexandria, alongside 3 other operatives. During this mission, he first came into contact with people who'd play important roles later in his life, the most important ones being Emilie Langley, a new hire but still more experienced than Kurotowa, and Cassandra Conway, the acting head of Kurotowa's group. Besides the three, a young Eric D'Angelo was attached to the group as a signal and communications specialist.

Kurotowa's cell would spend almost 3 years building a robust intelligence network to both maintain an eye on any attempts to intervene in post-war Elysia as well as prepare for the operation's ultimate goal of extracting confidential intelligence files from Albern's Staatsevidenzdienst headquarters. Kurotowa would come to make connections with his cell, becoming especially good friends with Langley. The two would be the ones carrying out the operation on the ground on March 15, 1919. Following the relative success of the operation, he and his cell would return to Elysia from Albern.

Kurotowa would remain in a domestic counter-intelligence role for the next few years, providing assistance to Conway and Langley in their respective cases when he had the time. During the Years of Oppression, Kurotowa would remain a dedicated operative for the MIS, with the closest treasonous act during the Civil War being providing intelligence to Conway, who acted as a double agent for the Revolutionary Guard. His unwitting assistance in this regard was enough for Conway to argue for a Protectorate pardon to be issued for Kurotowa. Kurotowa would continue working with Conway and Langley in piecing together the informant network utilized during the Reconstruction Period by the Unity State to follow various world events. This would continue until 1923, when he was transferred into the Office of Naval Intelligence and its 12th branch, MAJESTY.

## In ONI and MAJESTY

### Early Career and Rebellion

Kurotowa was assigned as aide-de-camp to Conway in her various operations. Besides the tasks assigned directly by Conway, Kurotowa was responsible for ferrying vital information from the global intel networks to the relevant departments within the government. He stayed much more under the radar compared to Conway, who began rising through the ONI's ranks. With her rise through the ranks, she began steadily becoming more politically involved, with Kurotowa accompanying her to many social events with other members of the party. Eventually, she began approaching members of her own department with ideas of rebellion, justifying the possibility with the ESU's treatment of those openly resisting the more oppressive policies of the government. Kurotowa, much more open to the idea, began assisting Conway in accruing more allies throughout the agency and wider military apparatus.

Kurotowa accompanied Conway during the ONI expedition into Hyperborea, as well as during the first set of talks between the ESU and Nichirin regarding the findings the Elysians had stumbled upon alongside the Nichirians. While Conway's meeting was nothing more than a thinly veiled attempt at blackmail, Kurotowa was attempting to maintain cordiality with the Nichirian delegation. Langley, while not directly involved in the meeting, was still a part of the delegation and began connecting the dots regarding what Conway was planning to do with the information the Elysians had gathered. Before she could definitively expose MAJESTY to the Nichirians during a meeting with Chief Liaison Officer [[akane-shikinami-hoffmann]], she was silenced by a Guangdongese mercenary acting on Conway's personal orders. Her assassination left Kurotowa conflicted, planting a seed of doubt about Conway's plans in his mind which would remain until his later defection from MAJESTY itself.

### As Vice-Director

Following Conway's framed disappearance on February 3, 1928, Kurotowa soon followed, alongside a large contingent of other MAJESTY operatives. He joined Conway in Shivon, overseeing the day-to-day operations and construction of the organization's first proper base. As Vice-Director, he would maintain a largely managerial role at the start; however, following events within the organization, he used what little power he had to move on to a role as a representative for the group, brokering deals with foreign governments and arranging contracts with private groups personally.

As time went on, Kurotowa steadily began to grow distrustful of Conway and her vision for MAJESTY, especially following the hiccups and resulting fallout of MAJESTY's false flag operation on behalf of Turulia. The final straw for him came on November 24, 1929, when Conway drugged him and subsequently sexually assaulted him while he was asleep. The event traumatized him greatly, leading him to take extended trips abroad while negotiating with foreign governments about his future plans

In August of 1930, he would return to MAJESTY's Rudanian base of operations for the last time to receive new orders directly from Conway, instructing him to find and recruit the people behind the recent political assassinations in Nichirin. Kurotowa, knowing such a plan was a certified death sentence, appealed to his close friend and confidant, Larry Foulke, regarding defection from the organization, asking him to also secure the well-being of the child Conway had conceived with him. He would arrive in Makinohara, Nichirin, in November of 1930, practically handing himself over to Nichirian authorities to broker a deal that secured the safety and future of him and those friendly to him within MAJESTY. It was thanks to the intel he provided that the Allied Forces would later be able to find and eliminate MAJESTY as a major threat.

## With SWB

Kurotowa would remain in Nichirian custody for some time afterwards, before ultimately being let go under a fake identity. He would live in a single-bedroom apartment in Shinkeisei for a few months to plan out his future. After some time of hammering out the details, he would decide to continue doing what he did in MAJESTY, although under a separate set of ideals and goals than what Conway had in mind, approaching the contacts he had remaining and, alongside Foulke, establishing a PMC under his oversight and management: Soldiers Without Borders, or SWB for short.

He would personally begin following up on leads on the locations of MAJESTY's other remaining leadership, finding and integrating their splintered remnants under his command and oversight. In 1934, the organization would finally make its presence known through an open letter sent to as many foreign governments and news agencies as it could.

## Personal Life

Kurotowa's life has necessitated him to often be on the move, meaning he's spent long periods of time in various residences across the globe. Currently, he lives in SWB's complex at [[mt-campbell|Mt. Campbell]], named after his late mother.

He maintains an active lifestyle, often accompanying those under his command on morning runs and, at other times, spending time at the gym. Despite this, he's an active smoker, often preferring to import cigars for his own use. He's also a fan of cinema, taking any possible excuse to visit one during trips to foreign countries.
