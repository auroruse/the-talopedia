**Zhang Yan** (張晏; born January 12th, 1904), codenamed **Burgundy**, is a Guandongese intelligence operative serving as the Head of Daedalus Operations in Valtheria. Born in Jinzhou during the Xing dynasty in Guandong, he was the son of two local actors. However, he was left without a family following an outbreak of TB in the area after the Koin War. He followed in his parents' footsteps for some time, earning money as a street performer and occasional actor, before turning to crime, taking bounty contracts from various criminal organizations. Eventually, he was recruited into Daedalus, who made the organization's main Valtherian theater operative. He's responsible for overseeing and coordinating Daedalus' intelligence operations across the continent, particularly adept at utilizing his talents as an actor to play multiple identities to deceive others.
