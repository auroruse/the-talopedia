**Robyn Tivruski** (born March 19th, 1898) is an Elysian politician and former researcher serving as the current Minister of Transport and Communications for the Elysian State of Unity under Supreme Protector [Archer Maine](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1Fe6bcre-u3qbes5A2N92ol9Rz89aOKpE9SHN1n5Ae5Y/edit) and [[arianne-malloran]]. As a certifiable prodigy, she achieved a Masters degree in Electrical Engineering at age 23. She first entered government service as a key member in the Infrastructural branch of the Elysian Reconstruction Authority, overseeing telegram and phone line reconstruction in Elysia and their connection into foreign countries. She served as head of the Elysian Telecommunications Service for some time afterwards before being appointed into her Ministerial position by the Supreme Protector in July of 1924. She is also notable for championing animal rights in Elysia, being the active chairwoman of the Elysian Pet Welfare Association. She is the youngest out of all of the members of the Politburo.
