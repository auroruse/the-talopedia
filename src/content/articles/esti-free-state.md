The Esti Free State, also known as Estiland, was a short lived nation in Northern [[evria]], bordering Livonia, Rudania and both the Karjanian Republic and the United Socialist States of Karjania during the [[karjanian-civil-war]]. It was proclaimed in the city of Toksavaara following the collapse of the [[karjan-confederation]] by Rauno Peetre, who led the state during it's entire existence. It was dissolved into the [[karjania|United Socialist States of Karjania]] on August 3rd, 1920. Nowadays, it's former area is divided into the Western Esti State and the Eastern Esti State within Karjania.

## History

The Esti Free State was proclaimed in Toksavaara in January, 1920, by members of the Esti Democratic Council in Toksavaara, which was led by writer Rauno Peetre. The state was recognized by a large number of nations, including both the Karjanian Republic and the U.S.S. of Karjania. The state was a parliamentary republic, it's first and only elections were held in the spring of 1920, where the Esti Freedom Party, led by Rauno Peetre claimed an overwhelming majority, taking 128 of the 150 seats. The Defence Forces of Estiland (DFE) saw combat in the Karjanian Civil War, and were disbanded after. The Esti Free State was majority Esti, containing approximately 78% of Esti people within the former Karjana Confederation, the remaining living in mostly Inkeria. The Esti Free State aligned itself with the democratic Karjanian Republic against the socialists, eventually losing the war, and being dissolved into the USSK. Following the civil war, the socialist government ran several assimilation campaigns in Esti majority regions.

## Government

The Esti Free State was a parliamentary republic, it's leader was the president of the state assembly, a role filled by Rauno Peetre throughout it's entire history, first as acting president before his party claimed a large majority in the 1920 elections. The Esti State Assembly was the main legislative body of the nation, which contained 150 seats. The only elections during it's history were held on March 19th, 1920. This resulted in an overwhelming victory for the Esti Freedom Party (EFP). Rauno Peetre, the leader of the EFP therefore continued his role as president of the assembly after his party took 128 seats. The remaining seats included 7 claimed by the Inkerian Movement (IM), 5 by the Esti Socialist Party (ESP), 3 by the Conservative Party of Estiland (CPE), 2 by the Union Party (UP) and 1 each by both the Esti Communist Party (ECP) and the Greens (G). A further 3 seats were taken by independent politicians. The Esti Free State was divided into 5 counties (Maakond), with the capital city of Toksavaara being administered separately. The currency of the state was the Kroon (Kr)

## Symbols

The main symbols of the Esti Free State were it's flag and coat of arms. The flag of Estiland dates back to the 17th century, and features 3 blue lions on a golden field, like the coat of arms. A blue stripe was added, making it a bicolour following the official constitution of the Esti Free State. The coat of arms consisted of 3 blue lions on a golden field, surrounded by oak branches. The Esti nobility derived this coat of arms from various Skjarnish symbols around the 15th century. It's blazon is the following: Or, three lions passant guardant azure, langued and armed gules

The anthem of the Esti Free State, Mu isamaa, mu õnn ja rõõm was written in the late 19th century by an Esti poet, and was sang to the tune of the former Karjanian anthem Maamme. Other symbols included the national animal, the lion, and  the state flower, the blue cornflower.
