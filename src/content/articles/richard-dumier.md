**Richard Dumier **(born March 19th, 1892) is an Elysian politician and former businessman serving as the current Minister of Labour for the Elysian State of Unity. Heir to a long-running family of businessmen and women, Dumier became CEO of Dumier & Co. following the death of his father in 1915, specializing in industrial machinery and electronics manufacturing. He made non-intrusion into people's backgrounds company policy, willing to hire people from a wide range of political affiliations, which, during a time of paranoia towards socialism, was a large risk. He would later get into trouble with the government, which would place him under arrest for suspected collaboration with communist agitators. The case would continue until the fall of the White Army in 1922. For his acumen in managing workers and his business background, Supreme Protector Archer Maine appointed him as Minister of Labour in July of 1922. Dumier often frequents the social circles of Elysia's media darlings, often present at movie premieres alongside his wife, actress Judy Dumier
