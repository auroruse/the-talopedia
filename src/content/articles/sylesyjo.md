**Grand Duchy of Sylesyjo** or just **Sylesyjo** is a country in Evria, laying between Rudania, Demaruz, Slytlia, Turulia and Cortesia. There are two rivers running through the country, those being Choroba and Nysa, which runs near the border. The country has roughly 40 million citizens, making it the 34th largest country on that scale. The capital city is Bramytolne, the largest city in the country and the 142th largest city in the world.

## Etymology

The name Sylesyjo comes from the name given by the Germanic Kingdoms, Silingi. Over time and labuages, the name evolved into what we can now call Sylesyjo.

The first mentions of Silingi come all the way from the V century.

The first documented use of Sylesyjo comes from 1356, when some dukes started joining the kingdom of Czechia.

## Histroy

The earliest sings of humans inhabiting the region come from 55000 years ago, which is not so far ago on the evolutionary scale. The oldest fossils were found in Slynsk and Bioly.

Around 5500 BC, we have confirmed signs that the people inhabiting the area started farming.

There was a mix of Celts, Germanics, Western Slavs and Lechites all in the area. They were building first cities, traded with each other and fought themselves. The earliest records of these  comes from the I century.

In the 10th century, the Polish Kingdom incorporated the area into their country.

Their rule over the region lasted roughly until 1241, when the Monglos invaded the country. Since the Poles were weakened by the invasion, Czechia, the neighbouring Kingdom, annexed Silesia, starting the era of czech rule that lasted nearly 5 centuries.

In 1742, the duchy was conquered by the Austrians. Their rule would last until 1835.

In 1835, the Duchy Declared itself Independent, finally ending its shuffling arround by foreign powers. It has stayed independent up to today

## Military

The Sylesyan Military is the official institution with their goal being to defend the nation from foreign threats.

They are split into 3 branches:

- Sylesyan Land forces
- Syleyjan Naval forces
- Sylesyan Air forces

## Goverment

The Grand Duchy of Sylesyjo is ruled by a Grand Duke. He has semi-absolute power although he has some restrictions placed on himself by the Constitution. He holds the executive power in the nation.

The legislative branch is made up of experts in many fields of science. They are writing, voting and sending laws to the Grand Duke. There are no elections into the parlament, the only deciding factor is your expertise.

The judicial branch works seperately from both of the institutions listed before. Nono of them may influence the working of this branch.

## Economy

The economy in the Grand Duchy of Sylesyjo is a mixed economy, where free market does exist while the State owns businesses as well.

The backbone of the Sylesyan economy is mining, mainly fossil fuels such as coal, oil and gas. Other major economic sectors are farming, arms manufacturing, vehicles manufacturing and many more. However, the Sylesyan economy is suffering from the lack of a large tech industry, leaving the nation in a disadvantage on that field.

## Geography

### Topography

The Sylesyan landscape is rather flat up north, while the southern side is hilly. The southeastern side has the highest peaks in the country. The highest mountain is Hoverla at 2061m

### Climate

The climate is very stable over the year, varying only by a few degrees per season. The temperatures go between -6 to 24 degrees Celcius

### Human Geography

Most metropolitan areas are built on the northern, flatter sides while smaller populations prefer living in the southern areas.

## Demographics

### Ethinc Groups

Most people living in Sylesyjo identify themselves as Sylesyans. There are however chunks of the population originating from the Neighboring states, such as for example Cortesia. There is also a lot of ethnic groups such as Tatars and Ruses

### Languages

The official language used in Sylesyjo is Sylesyan. It’s the only official language of this country.

There some regional languages aswell, the biggest of these being cyclic and Tatar

### Religion

The dominant religion in Sylesyjo is Christianity, with most of the country being catholic while a big part of the population is orthodox. Many young people, especially in big cities, are starting to turn away from religion and become atheist. The Tatar people are also muslim

### Citizenship

Citizenship is automatically given to anyone who has at least one parent with the Sylesyan citizenship. People who are immigrating to Sylesyjo and are currently in the process of getting citizenship may also give their children the Sylesyan citizenship.
