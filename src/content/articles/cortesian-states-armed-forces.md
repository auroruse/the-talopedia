The **Cortesian States Armed Forces** (CSAF) is the Federation’s joint military force and consists of three active branches: the Federal Cortesian Army (FCA), the Coastal Defense Navy (CDN), and the Cortesian Air Force (CAF). Additionally, a fourth reservist branch run independently by each of Cortesia’s member states is colloquially known as the Cortesian State Defense Forces (CSDF), though it is only to be called up in times of emergency or in direct defense of their respective state. All told, the CSAF numbers 1,667,040 personnel, with 1,111,360 service members within the active components of the CSAF and the remaining 555,680 service members within the reserve component of the CSDF.

The Cortesian States Armed Forces were established in their current form in April 1921 following the end of the Great War and the Cortesian War of Independence. Cortesia began a large undertaking of modernization immediately after winning their freedom, expanding their defense budget from 2.5% to a whopping 7% of the nation’s overall GDP, almost tripling their spending and giving the CSAF a new budget of roughly $150 billion per year. With this increase in funding, the CSAF has been expanding the military infrastructure through the nation, adding and modernizing bases while also further developing the technologies that work best with the rather flat topography of Cortesia; namely armored and mechanized ground forces alongside more advanced aircraft. Due to the limited nature of the nation’s coastline, however, the navy has been largely neglected, relegated to smaller, more outdated vessels compared to some of the more internationally-capable nations across Avium.

Also as part of their modernization and expansion efforts, Cortesia introduced a two-year mandatory service to all able-bodied males upon graduation from secondary education, though the first four to eight months are purely spent training conscripts to the same level as professional soldiers. Additionally, graduates are allowed an exception if they have arranged enrollment into a post-secondary education institution for a degree program of two years minimum. In this way, they hoped to both strengthen the nation’s education levels and grow the size of the CSAF. The first test of Cortesia’s modernized and expanded military was in 1930, in the Second Turulian War. The fighting was difficult and slow, as the Żelazny Mountains formed a barrier that the trucks and tanks of Cortesia had difficulty navigating. Dismounted infantry and aircraft made a huge impact, however, as well as a large amount of support among the local Cortesian and Nitravian populations.
