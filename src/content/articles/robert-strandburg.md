**Robert Strandburg **(born August 5th, 1890) is an Elysian lawyer and politician serving as the current Minister of Justice for the [[elysian-state-of-unity|Elysian State of Unity]] under Supreme Protector Archer Maine. Strandburg studied law at Eaton University, where he completed a Master of Laws, heading into employment as a private lawyer, defending multiple clients in the late 1910's and early 20's. A former classmate and friend of Archer Maine, he was appointed to the position of Justice Minister in July of 1922, and was instrumental in formulating the state's Constitution alongside Maine and Gestaltist Party Chairman Alyssa Traton.

Strandburg is also notable for spearheading the prosecution of detained MAJESTY operatives and collaborators, overseeing the trials of Thomas Binkowicz and Joseph Krul, and later Cassandra Conway personally. He is often noted by his fellow members of the Politburo for his work ethic, which he tends to take to the extreme, often affecting his Circadian rhythm negatively. He is married to Lise Strandburg, with whom he has a son.
