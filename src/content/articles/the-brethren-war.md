**The Brethren War** is the given name for the Elysian theater of the [[great-war|Great War]], fought from September 25th, 1914, to April 21st, 1916, in Eastern Elysia, the Acadian, as well as the Sahara Sea. It was primarily fought between the [[hollosend|Kingdom of Hollosend]], a member of the Winscor Accord, and the [[united|United States of Elysia]], a member of the [[eisenpakt]]. The war came about following decades of tension between Hollosend and Elysia, exploding following Hollosend's entrance into the Great War and the beginning of raids on Eisenpakt convoys. The USE, having been sympathetic to the Eisenpakt cause since the beginning of the war on the continent, officially denounced Hollosend's actions and declared war.

The Elysian entry into the war was expected by Hollosendian military command; however, they were surprised by the tenacity and speed at which the Elysians began advancing past their primary defensive lines at the border. The US Army managed to advance as far south as [[mempolis]], and eventually marched east, occupying [[annesburg]] and bypassing Hollosend's River Wall defensive network. Past this point, the front lines began to stabilize for Hollosend, with a network of trenches crisscrossing through the fields, swamps, and mountains of Eastern Elysia. Until 1916, both sides would commit to a series of costly offensive operations which saw very little tangible gain on either side. Meanwhile, at sea, the US Navy struggled to keep up with the mounting pressure in the Eastern Saharan Sea, running escort missions for Eisenpakt convoys while hounded by raiding parties from both the Hollosendian and Elvesterian Royal Navies. The naval campaign would ultimately come to an end during the decisive [[battle-of-the-sargassum-straits]], where a combined Elvesterian-Hollosendian battlegroup ambushed the US Navy's primary Battleship Division, sinking 1/3 of all ships and forcing the Elysians to *de facto* withdraw from the naval theater, only utilizing their navy for the defense of their direct coastal waters.

The Hollosendians would eventually break the state of pause on the front at the [[battle-of-annesburg]], utilizing Chlorine gas to shock and break through the Elysian defensive positions around the city. The move was heavily criticized abroad and domestically, with some Hollosendian officials seeing the use of chemical weapons as dishonorable, while others saw their use as necessary to end the war. The USE, to avoid being overrun or flanked in the chaos, moved to new defensive positions behind the newly repurposed River Wall defensive network, digging in once more for further Hollosendian attacks. By this point, both sides were veritably exhausted from the war, quickly entering into peace talks, eventually resulting in the signing of the [[remington-wesley-treaty|Remington-Wesley Treaty]], named after the Foreign Ministers of both nations, resulting in the direct exit of both Hollosend and the USE from the Great War. Both sides would, however, continue providing material support for their continental allies.

The war's inconclusive outcome was widely unpopular, with political actors utilizing it as material for their campaigning efforts. Particularly in Elysia, where former Army captain and business owner Vincent Stone would utilize a populist platform rooted in discontent with the war to take power, his actions eventually ignited the [[elysian-civil-war]] within the USE.
