**Avery Daniela Williams** (born March 23rd, 1906), codenamed **Crimson**, is an Elysian intelligence operative and military officer serving as the current head of Daedalus' Talons division. Born and raised in the rural wilderness around the Apalchen mountain range that surrounded her small home town, Avery was taught to hunt at a young age. During the Elysian Civil War, her home was razed by retreating White Army forces, leaving her homeless and an orphan. After drifting around the war-torn countryside for around a year, she was taken in by Daedalus operatives at the age of 16. She spent 4 years training under a multitude of instructors before being assigned to a cover job in the Ranger Corps. She is responsible for planning, and at times carrying out, assassinations of targets designated by the organization.

## Early Life

Avery Williams was born on March 23rd, 1906, in Lappo. A small rural town at the eastern foot of the Apalchen mountain range. The daughter of two local farmers, she was brought into the area's lifestyle at an early age, learning to handle a rifle before she learned to properly read. She became incredibly proficient at hunting wild game, a skill which would keep her family alive during harsher winters following the Brethren war.

Her family was relatively poor; the land that they owned having little in arable land, only really viable for grazing livestock. Through her parents' livelihood, she gained an understanding of how animals think and behave around people.

## Drifting and Recruitment

In 1921, during the intensification of the Elysian Civil War, retreating White Army forces would stop at her hometown to regroup and reorganize. The tension between the locals and the horde of soldiers suddenly appearing was palpable, eventually erupting once Guard-aligned Ranger units began ambushing White Army troopers at the outskirts of the town. In reprisal, the town was thoroughly looted, raided, and razed to the ground to prevent the town's use by the advancing Revolutionary Guard forces.

Williams, out on a hunt at the time, would come to find her home in ashes, and her family missing or executed in her childhood home's front yard. With the few possessions on her, including a camping kit and her family's old hunting rifle, she began wandering the countryside, drifting from small town to small town in search of jobs or anyone willing to pay her for the game she hunted. Her proficiency with a rifle was observed by Daedalus agents over a period of weeks, after which they would offer her proper training as an operative for the organization. With no other ties left, she took up the offer without hesitating.

## Daedalus Career

Her first years in the organization involved Williams being involved in a plethora of training programmes to mould her into a proficient field operative and assassin. She would rotate between training facilities for her first 4 years within the organization, graduating with flying colours and being assigned as a member of the Talons. As a cover, she was placed as a junior officer in the Fort Union Ranger Academy, rising in rank and eventually being promoted to 2nd Lieutenant by 1929, as well as being assigned as an instructor in the Ranger Academy.

In June of 1930, she would be assigned as the new head of the Talons, earning the codename Crimson as an inductee into the organization's "Red Cell" circle of operatives.
