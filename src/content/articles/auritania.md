**Auritania** (Auritania, *or-ih-TAY-nee-uh*), officially the **Glorious Republic of Auritania** (Gloriosa República de Auritania, *glo-RYO-sa reh-POO-blee-kah deh ow-ree-TAH-nee-ah*), is a country in Southern [[elysia]]. It borders [Hollosend](https://www.nationstates.net/nation=hollosend/detail=factbook/id=2681325) to the north and [Ponurvia](https://www.nationstates.net/nation=peanu-butteria/detail=factbook/id=2673635) through the [[aztekische-islands]], while having maritime access to the [[western-panthalassan-ocean]] to the west, the [[bay-of-anahuac]] to the southwest, the [[terranova-gulf]] to the northeast, and the [[sea-of-auritania]] to the southeast and processing a large lake, [[laks-nah|Lake Nahá]], and the northern portion of [[lake-tranquility]]. Auritania covers 2,931,723 km² (1,131,945 sq mi), and is the 6th largest country in the world by land area. With a population exceeding 141 million as of 1931, Auritania is the 10th most populous country in the world and is home to the largest number of native Askurian speakers. [[auritania-city]] is the capital and largest city in Auritania, which ranks among the most populous metropolitan areas in the world.

Human presence in Auritania dates back to at least 12,000 BCE in [[eastern-auritania]], although dates as old as 28,000 BCE have been proposed for less securely dated sites in [[western-auritania]]. Western Auritania, considered a cradle of civilization, was home to numerous advanced societies, including the [Tenocelome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olmecs), [Kukul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_civilization), [Tzapoteca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zapotec_civilization), [Teotlacatican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teotihuacan) civilization, and [[tarascan-empire|Tarasca]], while hunter-gatherer tribes and agrarian societies dominated Eastern Auritania. The most advanced among them was the [Mosca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muisca), who formed the [Mosca Confederations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muisca_Confederations_(political_units)). [[southeastern-auritania]] possessed the [[riwa-empire]], [Phuyusinchi Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chachapoya_culture), and [[inka-empire]], which were comparable to the civilizations of Western Auritania. Beginning in 1508, the [[izteca-empire]] under Emperor [[teuctzomacatl-ii|Teuctzocamatl II]] engaged in the [[huitzilopochtli-war]] with the Tarascan Empire. This culminated in 1518 with the Izteca Empire emerging as the sole hegemon of Western Auritania. This state of affairs didn’t last long however, as although managing to defeat the [[askurian-first-conquest-of-anahuac|Askurian first conquest]], the Izteca Empire collapsed in the years following Guatimozín assassination. After its collapse, dozen of kingdoms and empires were formed in the former imperial territory, before being unified by [[miyamictiani]] in 1531, forming the [[izteca-federation]]. Izteca, in the following decades, managed to reconquer lost imperial territories and established the [[quintuple-alliance]] in 1559 with the [[neo-tarascan-empire|Neo-Tarascan Empire]], [[cakchiquel-kingdom]], [[yuma-confederation]], and Inka Empire, politically uniting most of Auritania, before dissolving in 1590 after the Izteca Federation collapsed into a [[izteca-civil-war|civil war]]. Askurian  colonization began in 1593 with the [[askurian-conquest-of-the-inka-empire|conquest of Inka]] and the alliance with factions of the Izteca Civil War. As a response to this, the [[united-alliance-of-anahuac]] was formed in 1598 by [[north-izteca|North]] and [[south-izteca]] and the remaining native nations to resist Askurian colonization. The [[north-south-schism|North-South Schism]] in 1620 officially started the [[askurian-second-conquest-of-anahuac|Askurian second conquest]]. Although the Front managed to hold against Askurian conquistadors, it was ultimately defeated in the [[battle-of-karat|Battle of Karatá]] in 1639. By the late 17th century, most of Eastern Auritania had been colonized. The colony of [[new-askurias]], established in 1642 with its capital at [Tenochtepecan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenochtitlan), now Auritania City, became a major center of the transoceanic economy, fueled by silver mining and its position as a hub between [[evria]] and [[pelagonia]]. This gave rise to one of the largest multiracial populations in the world. The [[napolyonic-wars]] led to the 1810-1821 [[auritane-war-of-independence]], which ended Peninsular rule and led to the creation of the [[auritane-empire]], which collapsed in 1834 due to instability after Emperor [[orellana-i]] died of old age. In the west, the monarchy continued to rule, now reorganized into the [[first-anahuacan-empire]] under Orellana II, while in the east, the [[united-provinces-of-iglesia]] was established under President [[bol-var-juan|Bolívar Juan]]. Relations between Anahuac and Iglesia were very tense and border skirmishes between the two nations were common. After Orellana III passed away without an heir, a succession crisis occurred in Anahuac, which led to a [[anahuacan-civil-war|civil war]], military dictatorship and Hollosendian intervention, culminating in the establishment of the [[second-anahuacan-empire]] under the Hollosendian Emperor [[william-huntington-windsor|William Huntington-Windsor]]. In 1864, after a border accident, Iglesia declared war on Anahuac, starting the [[thousand-days-war]]. The war ended in 1867 with an Iglesian victory, annexing the entirety of Anahuac and forming the [[auritane-confederation]] with the new Constitution of 1867 and two capitals at [[auritania-city]], at the time known as Anahuac City, and [[botai]]. The decade after the war, later known as the Auritane Golden Age, saw great economic growth and the ascendance of Auritania into a regional power, even competing with Hollosend and the at the time [[united-states-of-elysia]], especially under [[gonz-lez-perfecto|González Perfecto]]’s presidency. The Confederation ended after the brief [[auritane-civil-war]] in 1876, from which General [[rafael-santos]] came to power and established the [[auritane-democratic-republic]]. The period later historians called Rafaelato saw stagnant economic growth, rising unemployment, rampant corruption, and severe social unrest due to Santos' dictatorship and his failed economic policies. Santos also centralised the government in Anahuac City, while brutally crushing any open rebellions against him and his regime, especially in Eastern Auritania. In 1910, [[fausto-i-maldonado|Fausto I. Maldonado]], an opposition to Santos in Western Auritania, rallied support and started the [[auritane-revolution]] in November. The Revolutionary Army managed to achieve a quick victory, forcing Santos to be exiled. Maldonado attempted to reform the failed economic policies of Santos and restore the Confederation, but was assassinated in a coup in 1913 by supporters of the old regime, led by Víctor Hidalgo, a former minister in Santos government. This restarted the civil war and led to a long power struggle inside the Revolutionary Army. Eventually, [[jaziel-cant|Jaziel Cantù]] won the power struggle in 1915. He managed to unite the Revolutionary Army and finally wiped out all oppositions and remnants of the old regime in 1920, uniting the entirety of Auritania.

Cantù declared the creation of the Glorious Republic of Anahuac on December 1, 1920, and started the [[first-nights-of-terror]] to consolidate power and eliminate all political opponents, even including his old allies. The event caused many in the public to question the regime, and resistance groups began forming, most notably [[free-auritania]], which was responsible for the [[march-21-uprising]]. As a response to the Uprising, Cantù started the [[second-nights-of-terror]], devastated the nation economically, while simultaneously implementing the Stabilization Reforms in 1925, beginning the centralization of political power around himself and his cabinet, the [[state-council-of-auritania|State Council]]. The decision was deeply unpopular with the public and members of Cantù’s government, leading to widespread protests and further instability. Drug cartels, most prominently the [[sinaloba-cartel]] in Western Auritania and the [[terranova-cartel]] in Eastern Auritania, utilized the chaos to expand their influence and power in rural areas. Realizing his mistakes, Cantù implemented the New Economic Plan in 1928 to fix the nation’s economy and regain public support, while also declaring the War on Drugs to combat the rise of drug cartels. The War on Drugs didn’t make much progress as of now, while the New Economic Plan has worked for the most part and Auritania’s economy has been recovering since then, thanks to the nation’s partial opening to the world, which officially happened in 1930. On April 1, 1931, Cantù issued a decree in front of the [[national-congress-of-auritania|National Congress]] to change the nation’s name and flag, resulting in the current official name and flag.

Auritania has a [totalitarian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totalitarianism), wherein all forms of political opposition are prohibited. The domestic press is highly regulated, and foreign literature is completely banned. The freedom of speech is severely limited, and people are constantly under surveillance by the Ministry of National Security. Anyone caught as a dissident or suspected to be one, no matter who they are, will be sent into working camps immediately without trial. Despite this, the state government is strangely tolerant to religions, which allow them to flourish. Auritania is a developing nation with low standards of living, educational quality, literacy, and life expectancy.

## Etymology

The name Auritania comes from [Rumaic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin), meaning “land of gold”, referring to the legend of [Aureus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Dorado), not to be mistaken as the city with the same name in Eastern Auritania. Auritania is also used to refer to the regions around the [[sea-of-auritania]] as a whole, which includes [[southern-elysia]] and [[western-pelagonia]]. Colloquially, Western Auritania is known as Anahuac, from the [Ahmatinatl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahuatl) name meaning “land surrounded by water”, and Eastern Auritania is known as Iglesia, after [[joel-de-iglesias]], the first Askurian explorer to reach the region. There are also hundreds of indigenous names for both regions as well.

Foreign exonyms have varied across languages and eras. Rumaic sources refer to Southern Elysia as a whole, including Auritania, as *Elysium* *Australissima*, meaning “Far Southern Elysia”, due to the fact that the Southern Elysian terrains and distance deter most Rumaic explorers and merchants at the time from reaching the land, although they did know about its existence and the peoples there through words and trades. The name changed to *Auritania* in the first century CE, when Rumaic explorers began hearing rumors about the legendary city of Aureus. This sparked interest in the land and led to many Rumaic expeditions into it. The [[zalekian-empire]] refers to Western Auritania as *Kumal* and its inhabitants, generally believed to be the [Kukuls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_peoples), as *Vel’Rethk*, meaning “blood people”, referring to the Kukuls’ human sacrifice, while Eastern Auritania is known in [[timla]] as *Temal* and its inhabitants, believed to be the [Mosca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muisca), as *Vel’Orak*, meaning “gold barbarians”.

When Evrian explorers arrived at Western Auritania in the early 1500s, they encountered the [[izteca-empire]] and the [Noza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexica), from which birthed the name *Nozania*. In 1566, a Murad official named Muhammed Ali Petrov led a group of colonists landed in [[southeastern-auritania]] and proclaimed the establishment of a colony for the [[murad-caliphate]] in the area, which he called *Petrostan*, after himself. The colony was short-lived due to the unfamiliar environment, lack of food and Inka warriors drove out Petrov and the Murad colonists within weeks, but the name Petrostan continued being used to jokingly refer to Eastern Auritania and its inhabitants well into the modern day.

Auritania was first used as a geographical term for both [[western-auritania|Western]] and [[eastern-auritania]] during colonial times. It entered official use through the [[auritane-empire]] in 1822, and continues being used to refer to the state that encompasses both Anahuac and Iglesia.

## History

### Native civilizations before First Conquest (pre-1519)

</div>

Native people inhabited the territory that is now [[eastern-auritania]] by 12,500 BCE. Nomadic hunter-gatherer tribes at the [El Blanco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Abra), [Ómito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tibit%C3%B3) and [Guasamisca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tequendama) sites near present-day Botai traded with one another and with other cultures from the [Yuma River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magdalena_River) Valley. The earliest human artifacts in Auritania are chips of stone tools found in the [Valley of Anahuac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valley_of_Mexico) in [[western-auritania]], dated to circa 10,000 years ago. Auritania is the site of the domestication of maize, tomato, and beans, which produced an agricultural surplus. This enables the transition from [paleo-Elysian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleo-Indians) hunter-gatherers to sedentary agricultural villages beginning around 5,000 BCE in Western Auritania and between 5,000 and 1,000 BCE in Eastern Auritania. This was also when pottery appeared, the earliest in Elysia. The formative period of Western Auritania is considered one of the cradles of civilization, this era saw the origin of distinct cultural traits such as religious and symbolic traditions, maize cultivation, artistic and architectural complexes as well as a [vigesimal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vigesimal) (base 20) numeric system that spread from Western Auritane culture to the rest of Auritania. Villages became more densely populated, socially stratified with an artisan class, and developed into [chiefdoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiefdom). The most powerful rulers had religious and political power, organizing the construction of ceremonial centers.

The earliest complex civilization in Auritania was the [Tenocelome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olmec) culture, which flourished at the southern banks of Lake Nahá from around 1,500 BCE, in the modern day province of [[villa-rica]]. Tenocelome cultural traits diffused through Western Auritania into other formative-era cultures in [[huaxyacac]], [[matinaktuun]] and the Valley of Anahuac. In the pre-classical period, the Kukul and Tzapoteca civilizations developed complex centers. Around this time, groups of Natives in Eastern Auritania, including the [Mosca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muisca), [Sinú](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zen%C3%BA) and [Bayaquim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quimbaya) developed the political system of [cacicazgos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cacicazgo) with a pyramidal structure of power headed by *caciques*. The first true Auritane writing systems were developed in the [Epi-Tenocelome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epi-Olmec_culture) and [Tzapoteca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zapotec_civilization) cultures at around 300 BCE, which spread to the rest of Auritania over centuries. The Eastern Auritane writing systems were drastically different from that of Western Auritania however, due to influence from [[timla]] through the Zalekian Empire and minor [Rumaic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin) influence from the Rumaic explorers entered the land in search for [Aureus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Dorado). The Auritane writing tradition reached its height in the Classic [Kukul Hieroglyphic script](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_Script) in 200 CE, the earliest written histories date from then. The tradition of writing was important after the Askurian conquest in 1621, with Native scribes learning to write their languages in alphabetic letters, while continuing to create pictorial texts.

In Western Auritania, the height of the classical period saw the ascendancy of [Teotlacatican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teotihuacan), which formed a military and commercial empire, known for trading with the [[zalekian-empire]], the [[rumaic-empire]] and the [[kalifornian-empire]], later the [[penutian-empire]]. Teotlacatican, with a population of more than 150,000, had some of the largest [pyramidal structures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesoamerican_pyramid) in Elysia. After the collapse of Teotlacatican around 600, competition ensued between political centers such as [Xōchicālco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xochicalco) and [Tēcālco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cholula_(Mesoamerican_site)). The [[riwa-empire]] emerged in the 7th century in [[southeastern-auritania]] and violently expanded into the surrounding territories, until it began to decline and collapsed in the 11th century. Around the 800s, the Mosca, who mainly inhabited the area of what is now the province of [[zaquiza]] and [[cunturquisa]] high plateau, formed the [Mosca Confederations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muisca_Confederations_(political_units)). They farmed maize, potato, quinoa, cotton, and traded gold, emeralds, blankets, ceramic, handcrafts, coca and especially rock salt with neighboring nations and the Zalekian successor states in the north, such as [[ohna]], [[hahtal]] and [[zantir]]. Interestingly, Mosca seem to have also sent their mercenaries to assist the successor states in their wars to make profits. At around the same time, the [Phuyusinchi Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chachapoya_culture) started and, despite being subjugated by the Riwa Empire, continued to exist until being conquered by the Inka Empire. During the Epi-Classic, [Ahmatina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahua_people) [peoples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahua_people) began moving south into Western Auritania, and became politically and culturally dominant in the region through the [Tlamatin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toltec) culture, while the modern day province of Hyaxyacac was dominated by the [Mixehca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixtec_culture) culture, and the lowland Kukul area had centers at [Chichén Huayá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chich%C3%A9n_Itz%C3%A1) and [Kukulpán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayap%C3%A1n). The [[black-death]] spread to Auritania in the 1350s and devastated the Native populations, which led to the collapse of many culture centers and civilizations, while also giving rise to new ones, such as Tarasca. The [Kickwa people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quechua_people) began migrated into Southeastern Auritania during the 1470s when the [[inka-empire]] expanded into the region, who became a major economic and military power in Eastern Auritania and become the main rival of the Mosca Confederation, later the Yuma Confederation.

Toward the end of the post-Classic period, two prominent civilizations were established in Western Auritania: the [[tarascan-empire]] and the [[izteca-empire]]. The Tarascan Empire, formed in the 14th century on the banks of [Lake Phásquaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_P%C3%A1tzcuaro) as an unify effort to combat the Black Death, was a centralised kingdom, perhaps the first truly [territorial state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Territorial_state) in Auritania, under the leadership of a *Irecha* (king) or *Kucha* (queen). The Izteca Empire, also known as the Triple Alliance, was formed in 1428 after the [[tepecan-war]], at first as an alliance of three Ahmatina city-states in the [Lake Hueyaltepec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Texcoco) region: the Noza’s [Tenochtepecan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenochtitlan), the Ndähiyo’s [Xōchitecpan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetzcoco_(altepetl), and the Tepecan’s [Cuauhtlān](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tlacopan). Each city-states has an emperor with their own advisors. However, through numerous imperial reforms, political powers gradually concentrated on Tenochtepecan and the Noza Emperor. This eventually culminated in Teuctzocamatl II’s imperial reform, where he demoted the two other emperors into viceroys and killed all of their advisors, while also making it so that no non-Noza people could enter nobility. The Tarascan Empire and the Izteca Empire were natural rivals as Tarasca blocked Izteca’s expansion to the north. They have fought numerous wars, collectively known as the [[flower-wars]], against each other, but most of them ended indecisively due to the Tarascan extensive border fortifications and its alliance with the peoples that the Izteca subjugated.

Everything changed in 1503 however, when Izteca managed to achieve a decisive victory in the [[battle-of-ir-kuaro|Battle of Irékuaro]], which severely weakened Tarasca’s military might. Realizing the opportunity, the newly ascended Emperor [[teuctzomacatl-ii|Teuctzocamatl II]], under the name of the god [Huitzilopochtli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hu%C4%ABtzil%C5%8Dp%C5%8Dchtli), mobilized large armies to finally defeat Tarasca for good. The [[huitzilopochtli-war]] began in 1515, when the Izteca army attacked Tarasca’s fortified southern border, which ended successfully with many Tarascan cities getting captured, but with high Izteca casualties, forcing the Izteca Empire into a temporary truce with Tarasca. However, it broke the truce after 2 years and attacked Tarasca again. Tarasca managed to resist the Izteca invasion and sometimes pushing it back, but they were struck by two natural disasters simultaneously for 2 years straight, an earthquake and a severe famine caused by a drought. Taking advantage of the chaos caused by the disasters, the Izteca Empire intensified its attacks, while also sending spies into Tarasca to spread suspicision in the Tarascan Court. An Izteca spy managed to convince Tzimtzincha II, the Irecha of the time, that his best general was plotting against him, which led to said general getting executed. The loss of its best general was Tarasca’s final nail in the coffin, as it couldn’t resist the invasion anymore and finally surrendered in 1518, and entirety of Tarasca was incorporated into the Izteca Empire as tributary provinces. With its rival gone, the Izteca Empire became the sole hegemon of Western Auritania and achieved its largest territorial expansion, encompassing most of Western Auritania. The Huitzilopochtli War struck fear in all of Auritania of a potential Izteca invasion. This fear was especially strong among nations in Eastern Auritania, such as the Mosca Confederation, which caused them to form a military alliance to protect themselves. Through the Askurian colonization of Navarelo and its first conquest of Anahuac, the alliance consolidated over time, eventually leading to the establishment of the [[yuma-confederation]] in 1540.

### Askurian First Conquest and Intermediate Era (1519-1613)

When the [[askurias]] established colonies in the [[gulf-of-hollosend]] in 1493, it made contact with the Auritane civilizations through [[joel-de-iglesias]] and his expeditions of 1500. At first, Askurias peacefully traded with the civilizations of Auritania by selling Evrian goods, such as foods, clothing, and iron weapons to both sides of the Huitzilopochtli War. After the war ended however, Askurias considered Anahuac to be its next target of colonization, especially after the establishment of [[navarelo]] in 1510. The [[askurian-first-conquest-of-anahuac]] began in 1519 when [[heliodoro-belmonte]], mayor of [[santiago-del-valle|Santiago del Valle]], capital of Navarelo, and captain of the Askurian expedition, founded [[villa-rica-city|Villa Rica]] and sought Native allies as his army marched toward Tenochtepecan. Although originally only had 630 men, Belmonte’s army swelled to thousands as more and more Natives who hated Izteca rule joined him, especially after he allied with the [[tlaxcallan-confederacy]], which sent 5,000-6,000 men to accompany him. Upon hearing of the news, Teuctzocamatl II quickly mobilized his army to defend Tenochtepecan. The two armies met outside the city and the [[battle-of-tenochtepecan]] began. Both sides suffered heavily, with Belmonte wounded and Teuctzocamatl II killed in battle. Belmonte’s army was forced retreated back to Villa Rica to recover and wait for Askurian  reinforcement, while Teuctzocamatl II’s brother Cuitlahuiatzin ascended to the throne, who quickly mobilized a large army to attack Villa Rica as revenge for his brother. Askurian reinforcement, under Pablo de Noguera, arrived at Villa Rica in 1520 and joined Belmonte’s army. However, conflicts between Belmonte and Noguera sparked when two of them disagreed on military tactics. This eventually culminated in Belmonte ordering his second-in-command Práxedes de Abascal to secretly assassinate Noguera. Although the assassination was successful, it was discovered by one of Noguera’s men, causing the Askuriam army to split with a majority of them stop following Belmonte’s commands. This was when the Izteca army, directly under Cuitlahuitzan, attacked. The following [[battle-of-nah-beach|Battle of Nahá Beach]] resulted in the defeat of the Askurian army and their allies, with Belmonte being killed and the surviving men fleeing back to Navarelo. Cuitlahuitzan would suffered the same fate as his brother when he was killed during battle. After his death, Teuctzocamatl II’s cousin Guatimozín ascended to the throne.

Although fending off the conquest, unrest spread throughout the Izteca Empire as more and more city-states ([*Altepetl*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altepetl)) under Izteca rule began to revolt, seeing that the Izteca authorities was severely weakened. Guatimozín attempted to follow his predecessors and control the situation, but was betrayed and assassinated by two of his advisors, Chicahuacpatlahuani and Tlilcahuitl. Guatimozín's death triggered a succession crisis within the Izteca Empire, as he didn’t have any heirs. Tlacotzin, one of Guatimozín’s generals, stepped up and established a regency until a new heir was chosen. Due to being inept, Tlacotzin often relied on Guatimozín’s advisors for advices. Utilizing this, Chicahuacpatlanhuani and Tlilcahuitl manipulated Tlacotzin to execute all other advisors and viceroys, started massive building projects, enlarged the army, increased taxes, and arrested messengers who delivered bad news. As a result, city-states across the realm stop obeying Izteca rule and instead declaring themselves kingdoms and empires, all while the independence movement led by Princess Eréndira consumed the Tarascan tributary provinces, eventually leading to the return of Tarasca under the [[neo-tarascan-empire|Neo-Tarascan Empire]].

During this time, Chicahuacpatlahuani and Tlilcahuitl came into conflict with one another, which culminated in Chicahuacpatlahuani persuading Tlacotzin to put Tlilcahuitl on trial, where he was ultimately executed. The worsening army situation then caused Tlacotzin to blame Chicahuacpatlahuani for the rebellion and executed him. As unrest continued to spread among the realm, Tlacotzin attempted to cling to the throne by proclaiming himself to be the true emperor with the reason that he was secretly a son of Teuctzocamatl II, thus proving his legitimacy. He was undermined by his ineptitude, and popular revolts broke out in 1525. When General [[miyamictani|Miyamictiani]], a former civil service worker, and his army besieged Tenochtepecan, a state in such turmoil couldn’t hold for long. Tlacotzin surrendered to Miyamictiani upon the latter's arrival at the [[palace-of-the-emperor]] in 1527. While initially being spared by Miyamictiani, he was executed shortly after by Miyamictiani’s men. In 1527, Tenochtepecan was set ablazed, marking what historians consider to be the end of the Izteca Empire. With the former Izteca territories temporarily divided into the dozen of kingdoms and empires before Miyamictiani reunite them. On February 26, 1531, in the new capital of [[omilpan]], Miyamictiani declared himself emperor of the newly founded [[izteca-federation]].

Miyamictiani believed centralization was the root of all tyranny, and was partially inspired by ideas of the old [[zalekian-republic]]. The kingdoms and empires still exist but as semi-autonomous regions in the Federation, each ruled by the same rulers as before, but now they were called commanderies. The commanderies formed a council for state affairs and had the ability to appoint and depose an emperor if they thought it was necessary. Although the idea was considered radical for Miyamictiani’s time, the people accepted it thanks to his charisma. In his 7 years reign, Miyamictiani had rebuilt the Federation economically and militarily back into a regional power and settled border disputes with neighboring nations, resorting to war when necessary. After Miyamictiani passed away, the new emperor Tlahuizmictiani continued to stabilise the Federation, while also repairing relations with neighboring nations. This eventually culminated in the formation of the [[quintuple-alliance]] under his reign in 1559. The decades after the formation of the Alliance saw a long period of peace and prosperity in Auritania, thanks to the cooperation of the member nations economically and militarily. The Alliance was notable for fending off multiple Elvesterian expeditions into Auritania, as after the [[war-of-jerkins-ear|War of Jerkins’ Ear]], [[elvester]] had seized the control of Navarelo from Askurias and had sought to expand its influence into Auritania. One such example was the [[yuma-war-of-1569]].

Over time, factionism began forming in the commandery council as disagreements over state affairs became more frequent, while the emperors themselves attempted to consolidate more political power away from the council. This eventually reached a boiling point in 1588, when the Inka Empire, a member of the Quintuple Alliance, collapsed into civil war due to a succession crisis after its [[sapa-inka]] passed away. The commanderies and Emperor Cuallitemīcti were split on how to respond to the civil war, with one side wanting to intervene while others preferred to stay neutral. This caused Cuallitemīcti to attempt to arrest the council for treason but failed, leading to the commanderies raising armies against the emperor. Although the commanderies intended to just kill Cuallitemīcti and replace him with a new emperor, disagreement quickly appeared among them about who would be the new emperor and suddenly, the commanderies were now fighting themselves, thus starting the [[izteca-civil-war]] as federal authority collapsed across the realm. With it, the Quintuple Alliance, without its leader, collapsed alongside Izteca, ending an era of prosperity for the civilizations of Auritania.

## Military

The [[glorious-republic-armed-forces]] (*Gloriosas Fuerzas Armadas de la República*) are administered by the Ministry of the Armed Forces (*Ministerio de las Fuerzas Armadas*). There are three branches: the [[glorious-republic-army]] (*Glorioso Ejército de la República*), the [[glorious-republic-air-force]] (*Gloriosa Fuerza Aérea de la República*), and the [[glorious-republic-navy]] (*Gloriosa Armanda de la República*). The [[national-guard-auritania|National Guard]] (*Guardia Nacional*) functions as a [gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gendarmerie), while responsible for law enforcement, it is placed under military command. The Glorious Leader and the Minister of the Armed Forces act as Commander-in-Chief of the military. According to official government reports, Auritania spent roughly $215 billion on defense in 1930, accounting for around 6% of GDP.

The total strength of the Armed Forces is ~2.1 million personnel in active duty, with 1.2 million in the Army, 63 thousand in the Air Force, 210 thousand in the Navy, and 672 thousand in the National Guard, with an additional 700 thousand trained in the reserve. Technologically, the Armed Forces are quite backward compared to the rest of the world, especially in the Air Force and the Navy, but modernization efforts are improving the situation, especially after the War on Drugs started.

The current military of Auritania was formed from the Revolutionary Army in 1922. As of now, the military has only participated in the currently ongoing War on Drugs, while remaining neutral in international conflicts. Currently, there is no known plan for the military to participate in any conflicts outside of Auritania in the near future.

## Government

The Glorious Republic of Auritania is a [totalitarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totalitarianism) [communist state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communism). Its system is based on [unified state power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unified_state_power) as well as [democratic centralism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_centralism). Political power is highly centralized in the office of the [[glorious-leader]], who exercises supreme authority over both the state and the ruling party. Executive authority is vested in the Glorious Leader and the [[state-council]], whose members are ministers appointed directly by him. The executive possesses broad legislative powers, including the ability to introduce laws and override existing legislation through executive decrees. In practice, these decrees supersede other governmental processes.

The [[glorious-party-of-auritania]] (*Gloriosa Fiesta de Auritania;* GFDA), formally known as the Revolutionary Party of Auritania and the Glorious Party of Anahuac, functions as the state’s sole legal political party and serves as its ideological foundation. However, its role has diminished over time, and it largely operates as a formal or symbolic body. The [[politburo-of-the-glorious-party-of-auritania|Politburo]] is officially designated as the highest decision-making institution within the party-state structure, though its influence has declined significantly in comparison to the executive leadership. Legislative functions are formally carried out by the [[national-congress-of-auritania|National Congress]], a unicameral body composed of representatives from regions, labor, and military sectors. The Congress is responsible for passing legislation, approving the national budget, and formally electing key officials. In practice, it operates under significant state direction and primarily serves to ratify executive decisions, legitimize political transitions, and disseminate official policy.

The state maintains an extensive internal security apparatus. The Ministry of National Security conducts surveillance of both civilians and government officials, including members of the ruling party, with regular loyalty assessments aimed at identifying dissidents. Judicial authority is divided between the Ministry of Justice, which oversees standard legal matters, and the Ministry of National Security, which independently handles cases related to political offenses. Proceedings in such cases are typically conducted outside the conventional judicial framework.

The current government of Auritania is the result of the Stabilization Reforms it has undergone since 1925. The Reforms have led to the centralization of political power around the Glorious Leader and the State Council. The Reforms also led to the replacement of many communists in the government with loyalists.

Administratively, Auritania is divided into 1 Special Capital Zone and 55 provinces with 3,283 municipalities.

**Special Capital Zone** (*Zona Capital Especial*) encompasses the entirety of Auritania City, known as the [[auritania-city-scz|Auritania City SCZ]]. It is a special administrative region and has no intermediate provincial-level administration, instead being directly governed by the national government. This is because of Auritania City’s economic importance and its status as the capital and the largest, most populous city in Auritania.

**Provinces** (*provincias*) are the standard administrative units of Auritania. Each is headed by a Provincial Governor (*gobernador provincial*), who is appointed by the Ministry of the Interior (*Ministerio del Interior*). Provinces are divided into municipalities, which continues to get divided further.

**Municipalities** (*municipios*) are the second-level administrative divisions of Auritania and are below provinces. Each is headed by a Municipality President (*presidente* or *presidenta del municipio*, depending on the president’s gender), who is also appointed by the Ministry of the Interior, like the Provincial Governors. Municipalities are divided into localities (*localidades*), which get divided further into boroughs (*distritos*) and neighborhoods (*colonias* and *barrios*, they are distinct from each other). In some provinces, municipalities are grouped together into regions (*regiones*) for geographical, cultural or political reasons.

In Auritania, municipalities should not be confused with cities. Cities (*ciudades*) are locality-level divisions, which are below municipalities, although some larger cities are consolidated with their own municipalities and form a second level of governance, underneath provinces. In addition, the 16 boroughs of Auritania City are considered municipalities equivalent. This makes the total number of municipality-level divisions to be 3,299.

## Economy

Auritania is a [newly industrialized country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newly_industrialized_country) and has a [developing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Developing_country) [command economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Command_economy) under the authority of the Ministry of the Economy, whereby production and distribution of goods are centralized and directed by the government. It is the 5th largest in Elysia and the 16th largest in the world by [purchasing power parity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purchasing_power_parity), with a [gross domestic product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_domestic_product) of $3.6 trillion as of 1930. The economy of Auritania was based on state ownership of the means of production, collective farming, and industrial manufacturing. An administrative-command system managed a distinct form of central planning. The Auritane economy is characterized by state control of investment, prices, a dependence on natural resources, lack of consumer goods, shortage of goods and services, little foreign trades, public ownership of industrial assets, macroeconomic stability, low unemployment, high job security, low inflation, but with high poverty and high economic inequality.

Auritane industrial sectors are heavily concentrated in urban centers, such as [[auritania-city]], [[botai]] and [[cual-coyotl|Cualācoyotl]], while the rest of the nation remain agrarian. This created large gaps between the urban and rural population, the northern and southern provinces, and the rich and the poor. The avocado and coffee industries are the backbone of the economy, with sub-industries such as food processing, livestock, fishing, oil, mining, silver, gold, emerald and coal accounting for the majority of Auritania’s economic output. In recent years, the economy has seen a rise in automobile and electronics manufacturing, especially after the New Economic Plan was implemented. Certain provinces in Auritania are specialized in an economic sector, such as [[michalli|Michcalli]] and [[guevara]] for avocado and coffee growing, [[tajo]] for livestock processing, [[zalaya]] and [[holgu-n|Holguín]] for gold and silver mining, and recently [[auritania-city-scz|Auritania City SCZ]], [[cunturquisa]] and [[xaltipac]] for automobile and electronics manufacturing.

Extensive bases of natural resources in Auritania further fuel its economic growth. [[western-auritania]], the nation's industrial heartland, extracts vast quantities of silver, copper, zinc and lithium, while [[eastern-auritania]] contributes vast quantities of gold, coal and emerald. Eastern Auritania also contains vast swathes of fertile soil, allowing lucrative cultivation of agricultural commodities, most notably coffee, fresh-cut flowers and tropical fruits, with rice and cocoa beans in the provinces of [[rimachu]] and [[sacharuna]], while also allowing for massive livestock raising and processing. The [Sierra Padre Occidental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Madre_Occidental) and the [[gargarea-rainforest]] represent Auritania’s primary timber harvesting center, although there are concerns of unsustainable logging in those areas. Having access to the [[western-panthalassan-ocean]] and the [[sea-of-auritania]] create the thriving fishing industry of Auritania, yielding large catches of shrimp, bass, tuna, sardines, lobster, and abalone, while [[lake-nah|Lake Nahá]], [[lake-tranquility]] and numerous rivers within Auritania provide tilapia, carp, trout, catfish, tetras, cichlids, discus, stingrays and arowana. The [Tlilchapopotli Fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicontepec_Field) in Western Auritania, alongside the [Callisto León Oilfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ca%C3%B1o_Lim%C3%B3n) in Eastern Auritania, represents Auritania’s primary petroleum production centers, although the technology to extract them is still limited. Recent discovery of the [Carmona Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantarell_Field) in the [[bay-of-anahuac]] has prompted the government to invest in offshore oil extraction technology.

### Labor Force

Auritania’s domestic labor force in 1930 totaled 68.8 million people, accounting for approximately 49% of its population. During 1930, 62% of the domestic labor force worked in the primary sector (agriculture, food processing, mining, oil, etc.); 25% in the secondary sector (forestry, fishing, manufacturing, etc.); and 13% in the tertiary sector (banking, administration, etc.). The unemployment rate in 1930 was estimated at 2.7%. In 1930, approximately 36.3 million people were employed by various state companies with most of them joining a labor union.

The Auritane labor force is characterized by state-controlled employment. The government mandates all adults, regardless of their gender, to get employed and they would automatically be granted union membership when doing so. This helps Auritania achieve higher employment rates and female employment than other countries in Elysia respectively. Most Auritane workers enrolled in one of the 30 labor unions overseen by the Ministry of the Interior, the largest and most popular among them was the Confederation of Auritane Workers (*Confederación de Trabajadores Auritanos;* CTA). While union membership brought benefits like holiday vouchers, these organizations served to enforce state directives rather than protect independent worker rights. Outside the civilian workforce, the government also used large-scale forced labor from prisoners of working camps as an economic tool, especially in dangerous jobs such as mining. The exact number of those forced laborers remains unclear, although estimatedly it could reach hundreds of thousands or even millions.

### Topography

Auritania is located in the southern portion of [[elysia]], with a total area of 2,931,723 km² (1,131,945 sq mi), it is the 6th largest country in the world by land area. It has coastlines on the [[western-panthalassan-ocean]], the [[bay-of-anahuac]], the [[terranova-gulf]] and the [[sea-of-auritania]]. Almost all of Auritania lies on the Elysian plate, with small parts on the Western Panthalassan plates.

There is also the [Auguasnegras Structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vichada_Structure), a large impact crater in the northeastern province of [[aguasnegras]], which belongs to the Llanos. Although Auritania is large, much of Western Auritania’s landmass is incompatible with agriculture due to aridity, soil or terrain. Eastern Auritania is more agricultural however, supporting a wide range of crops and fruit trees.

In 1930, an estimated 46.5% of land was agriculture; 7.1% was arable; 3% was in permanent crops; 37.4% was permanently pasture; and 42.7% was forest. Auritania was irrigated by several rivers, with the longest being the [Yuma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magdalena_River), which served as a natural border between Western and Eastern Auritania, along with Hollosend. Auritania also possesses [[lake-nah|Lake Nahá]], which has numerous economic benefits for the surrounding provinces, and the northern portion of [[lake-tranquility]].

### Climate

The climate of Auritania is varied due to the country’s size and topography. Western Auritania’s temperatures are fairly consistent year-round and vary solely as a function of elevation. Eastern Auritania is characterized for being tropical, presenting variations depending on the altitude, temperature, humidity, winds and rainfall.

There are six major climate types in Western Auritania, with warm sub-humid climate being coastal up to 900 m (2,953 ft); temperature humid and sub-humid being found mostly on pastures at an elevation of 1,800 m (5,906 ft) and higher and cold climate usually found at an elevation of 3,500 m (11,483 ft) and glacial climate above 4,000 m (13,123 ft). Most of the Western Auritania’s territory has a dry climate. Eastern Auritania has a diverse range of climate zones, including tropical rainforests and savannas. About 82.5% of Eastern Auritania’s total area lies in the [warm altitudinal zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tierra_caliente).

Areas of Western Auritania with elevations up to 1,000 m (3,281 ft) and the southern parts of the coastal highlands have a yearly median temperature between 24 and 28°C (75.2 and 82.4°F). Temperatures here remain high throughout the year, with only a 5°C (9°F) difference between winter and summer median temperatures. The Panthalassan Coast and the [[bay-of-anahuac]] is subject to natural hazards such as tsunamis and is vulnerable to serious [hurricanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_cyclone) during the summer and fall. [Mountain climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_climate) is one of the unique features of the Riesengrat Cordilleras and other high altitude reliefs where temperature is determined by elevation. Below 1,000 m (3,281 ft) in elevation is the [warm altitudinal zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tierra_caliente), where temperatures are above 24°C (75.2°F). The [temperate climate altitudinal zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tierra_templada) located between 1,001 m and 2,000 m (3,284 and 6,562 ft) is characterized for presenting an average temperature ranging between 17 and 24°C (62.6 and 75.2°F). The [cold climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tierra_fr%C3%ADa) is present between 2,001 and 3,000 m (6,565 and 9,843 ft) and the temperatures vary between 12 and 17°C (53.6 and 62.6°F). Beyond lies the alpine conditions of the forested zone and then the treeless grasslands of the [páramos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C3%A1ramo). Above 4,000 m (13,123 ft), where temperatures are below freezing, the climate is [glacial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tierra_helada), a zone of permanent snow and ice.

### Human Geography

Auritania’s population distribution closely follows its topography. The majority of people reside within the Anahuacan Plateau and Riesengratian highlands, where dense urban centers have developed due to favorable terrain and climate. Western and Central Auritania served as an economic and demographic center, which contrast sharply to the more rural and traditionally indigenous Southern and most of Eastern Auritania. The majority of Auritania’s population is concentrated in urban areas, such as [[auritania-city]], [[botai]] and [[cual-coyotl|Cualācoyotl]], while the rest of the country is sparsely populated. The national population density was approximately 64.43 people per square kilometer in 1930, placing it in the mid-range among developed nations. However, this average belies massive regional contrasts. Heavily urbanized metropolises typically record densities exceeding 3,000 people per square kilometer, while rural areas generally fall below 12 people per square kilometer. Urbanization continues to rise steadily, caused by rural migration into government-designated cities where industrial employment and public works offer new economic opportunities. Major cities, especially Auritania City, Botai, Cualācoyotl, Yoloxochitl, and Los Ángeles, have absorbed the bulk of this migratory influx, expanding rapidly as industrial hubs and administrative centers over the past decade.

## Demographics

### Ethnic Groups

Auritania is ethically diverse, its people descending from the original Native inhabitants, Askurian conquistadors, Pelagonians originally brought to the country as slaves, and 19th century immigrants from Evria and Nabatea, all contributing to a diverse cultural heritage. The demographic distribution reflects a pattern that is influenced by colonial history.

At Auritania’s 1930 census, 26.3% of the population identified as Native and 8.3% of Auritania’s population speaks an indigenous language. Whites (Evrians) and Pelagonians were 18.4% and 3.1% of Auritania’s population respectively, while Nabateans and Valtherians were around 1% of the population each.

### Languages

[Askurian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_language) is the [de facto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_facto) national language spoken by the vast majority of the population, making Auritania the world’s most populous [Askurophone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanophone) country. [Anahuacan Askurian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Spanish) and [Iglesian Askurian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_Spanish) refers to the varieties of Askurian spoken in Western and Eastern Auritania, which differ from one region to another in sound, structure, and vocabulary. There is also [Riesengratian Askurian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andean_Spanish) and [Gargarean Askurian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazonic_Spanish), which are mainly spoken in Southeastern Auritania.

The state government recognizes one hundred and fifty-six linguistic groups and 888 varieties of indigenous languages, with two [Creole languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creole_languages) in addition. In 1930, an estimated 11.7 million citizens spoke these languages. [Ahmatinatl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahuatl) is spoken by more than 2.3 million, followed by [Matinaktuun Kukul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yucatec_Maya_language), used daily by nearly 1.1 million people. [Tequi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tzeltal_language) and [Tzinac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tzotzil_language), two other [Kukul languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayan_languages), are spoken around one-half million people each, primarily in the southwestern province of [[chian]]. [Mixehca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixtec_languages) and [Tzapoteca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zapotec_languages), with an estimated 700,000 native speakers each, are two other prominent language groups. Through the National Indigenous Languages Institute and the General Law on the Linguistic Rights of Native Peoples, all indigenous languages were recognized as [de jure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_jure) “national languages” with status equal to that of Askurian. That notwithstanding, in practice, Native Peoples often face discrimination and do not have full access to public services such as education and healthcare, or to the justice system, as Askurian is the prevailing language. However, the state government is actively trying to change this, while also preserving endangered languages through Language Day, celebrated on April 23 every year.

Aside from indigenous languages, there are several minority languages spoken in Auritania due to international immigration, such as [Nordalemannsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plautdietsch) by the 80,000-strong Nordmarken population, primarily settled in the southwestern province of [[gran-torre]]. The [Choquiliztlan dialect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chipilo_Venetian_dialect), a variant of the [Venesian language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venetian_language), is spoken in the town of Choquiliztlan, located in the western province of [[los-ngeles|Los Ángeles]], by around 2,500 people, mainly descendants of Venesians that migrated to the area in the late 19th century.

[Brantish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_as_a_second_or_foreign_language) is the most commonly taught foreign language in Auritania. It is estimated that nearly 28 million, or around a fifth of the population, study Brantish through public schools, private institutions, or self-access channels. However, in 1930, a high level of Brantish proficiency was limited to 5% of the population. [Avernois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language) is the second most widely taught foreign language. Every year, between 250,000 and 300,000 Auritanes students enroll in Avernois language courses.

### Religion

Although the Constitutions of 1867 and 1920 put limits on the role of the Ecclesiastical Nazarist Church in Auritania. [Ecclesiastical Nazarism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church) remains Auritania’s dominant religious affiliation. In the 1930 census, 70.3% (99,484,342) of the population were Ecclesiastical Nazarist. 22.1% (31,247,594) belong to [Veritism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism). 6.2% (8,773,868) of the population declared [no religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atheism) and 1.4% (1,981,196) belonged to other religions.

The 130,731,936 Nazarists of Auritania constitute in absolute terms the largest Nazarist community in the world. In 1930, 63.0% of them attended church services weekly. Migratory phenomena have led to the spread of different aspects of Nazarism, including branches Veritism, [Northern Ecclesiastical Nazarist Churches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Catholic_Churches) and [Northern Sorovite Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church).

In the 1930 census, there were 237,270 Hasmoneans in Auritania. The presence of Hasmoneans in Auritania dates back to the 16th century when Askurian arrived in the region. The modern Hasmonean Community began to be formed in the late 19th century when Hasmoneans from Evria and the Murad Caliphate immigrated to Auritania due to instability and antisemitism. [Shahadism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam) in Auritania, with 32,167 members, is practiced mostly by Nabateans. In the 1930 census, 148,159 Auritanes belonged to a spiritualist religion, a category which includes a tiny [Shugendōsha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism) population. About 298,220 people practiced religions with “ethnic roots”, religions of mostly Pelagonian and Native origin.

There is often a [syncretism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syncretism) between [shamanism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shamanism) and Nazarist traditions. A religion of popular syncretism in Auritania is [[unisolura]], which is very prominent in the south, with its sanctuary in [[villa-del-sol|Villa del Sol]]. One of the more exemplary cases of popular religiosity is the cult of Holy Dead ([*Santa Muerte*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Muerte)). Other examples are the representations of the [Passion of Christ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stations_of_the_Cross) and the celebration of [Day of the Dead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Day_of_the_Dead), which take place within the framework of Nazarist imagery, but under a very particular reinterpretation.

### Citizenship

Auritane citizenship is based on the principle of [jus soli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jus_soli). Any person born within the national territory automatically receives citizenship regardless of their parents' nationality or ethnic background. This birthright citizenship doesn’t extend to any person born abroad, even if one or both of their parents are Auritane. Any foreigners who want to have Auritane citizenship must have residency in Auritania for at least 5 years and complete a test to prove their knowledge of the Oestenic language and Auritania history, along with their ideology and allegiance to the state.
