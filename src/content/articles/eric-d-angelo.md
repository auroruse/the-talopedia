**Eric Michael D'Angelo** (born September 2nd, 1900), codenamed **Silvereye**, is a Vicilian-Elysian intelligence operative and criminal investigator serving as the current head of Daedalus' Domestic Operations department. Born and raised in Axiom's Little Vicily district, D'Angelo was enlisted in the Maplestone Military Academy as a prospective recruit after running away from home, and was quickly inducted as a signal specialist. He was practically immediately assigned as the communications specialist for Operation Alexandria. Following the operation's success, he signed up for retraining, being inducted into the "Chem-aug" program; however, he suffered a negative reaction to the formula used and lost his left eye. Because of this, he assumed a lighter counter-intelligence role in Elysia, eventually being promoted to the ONI's Head of Ground Operations following Cassandra Conway's defection. D'Angelo oversees and organizes operations on Daedalus' home turf, using the State Bureau of Intelligence as his cover for these operations. He's a proficient gunslinger and talented investigator, which he uses to great effect in his cover job.

## Early Life

Eric D'Angelo was born on September 2nd, 1900, in Axiom's Lower Metropol's Little Vicily, to Vicilian-Elysian parents. His parents were poor, forcing Eric to take up small jobs around his neighborhood to earn some amount of pocket change. This would often be forcibly stolen by his father, a strict and violent man, for ventures that would rarely, if ever, end up paying off. He was much more attached to his mother, who would often assist him in looking for new jobs and generally teach him respectful manners. Due to his habits, he would often get into trouble with the adults and authorities in his neighborhood.

Eventually, he grew fed up with this constant cycle, eventually running away from home at the age of 14. He drifted through the streets and the criminal underworld of Axiom for around a year until he caught the attention of agents of Daedalus. He was offered a place to stay, training, and a permanent job, the prospect of which made him accept almost immediately.

## Career in Daedalus

D'Angelo would be transferred to the Maplestone Military Academy for training as an operative for Daedalus, becoming well-versed in many tasks and professions expected of capable field operatives for the organization. Following the Remington-Wesley Treaty in 1916, D'Angelo was assigned to Cassandra Conway's cell operating in Alemannia as part of Operation Alexandria as a signal specialist, handling radio intelligence, as well as situational updates to other cells and the U.S.E. Recognizing his talents in actual field operations, Conway would recommend D'Angelo be inducted into the U.S.E.'s nascent "Chem-Aug" program, a subject Conway had been for years before.

Following intensive survival and field training, D'Angelo's time in the program would come to a screeching halt after an intense reaction he suffered to the compounds used in the Chem-Aug program's chemical formula, Nausol. The reaction was severe enough for him to lose the use of his left eye, swiftly replacing it with a glass eye. His potential as a talented field agent was squandered by his reaction, relegating him to operating primarily as a counterintelligence operator. He would remain in this role even after the Civil War, able to escape persecution by the new Gestaltist government by using his connections within Daedalus.

D'Angelo would continue operating as an investigator for the nation's new government, working first for the Civil Guard, and later the State Bureau of Intelligence following the intelligence reforms in the latter half of the 1920's. He would act as an important investigator and interrogator during the MAJESTY Crisis, interviewing multiple MAJESTY operatives and associates, including the former Turulian president, Bartek Kaminski, mere weeks prior to his death.
