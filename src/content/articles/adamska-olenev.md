**Adamska Ilyich Olenev** (born June 6th, 1899), codenamed **Garnet**, is an Elysian-Rudanian intelligence operative and hitman serving as the Head of Daedalus Operations in Evria. Born in Kurchask to a Rudanian father and an Elysian mother, Adamska spent most of his early childhood running from authorities with his mother due to the political affiliations of his parents. He would be sent back to his mother's homeland at the age of five, where he would be intercepted by Daedalus agents posing as friends of his parents. He would be inducted into the organization's various training programs, performing well enough to act as an instructor to new agents, until 1920, when he would be transferred to Rudania as a member of the burgeoning Red Army. His skill in marksmanship and tactical know-how would enable him to skyrocket through the ranks, eventually reaching the rank of Major by age 31. Olenev is a longtime fan of Western films, often sporting attire stereotypical of Western outlaws, with his weapon of choice being an Elysian Single Action Army revolver. He oversees and organizes Daedalus operations in mainland Evria.
