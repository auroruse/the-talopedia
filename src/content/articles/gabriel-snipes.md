**Gabriel Wesley Snipes** (born June 12th, 1897) is an Elysian politician and former military officer serving as the current Minister of the Interior of the Unity State. He previously served as the aide-de-camp to Archer Maine during the Elysian Civil War, before entering politics as a founding member of the Gestaltist Party of Elysia. He also previously served as a military policeman in service of the United States of Elysia, before being horrified by the state’s crimes against its people and joining Archer Maine in establishing the Revolutionary Guard of Elysia. As a part of his role as Minister of the Interior, he oversees the Unity State’s surveillance apparatus, as well as its police forces. He is the second youngest member of the Politburo.
