The Karjanian Civil War was an armed conflict fought in 1920 for control of [[karjania]] following the collapse of the Karjan Confederation. The two main factions were the socialists (reds) and the republicans (whites). It begun on the 8th of January, 1920 following the proclamation of the Pietari Commune in [[pietari]] by Yrjö Sinnar, who led the reds and later became chairman of the Karjanian Communist Party. The republicans were led by Heikki Aaltonen, a former noble in the Council of Nobles, who was the sole survivor of the Palace Massacre on the 9th of January in Pietari. He officially proclaimed the Karjanian Republic later in January, in Roudanjärvi. Esti revolutionary Rauno Peetre took control of the majority Esti speaking lands, and declared independence as the Esti Free State. The Saamit Socialist State, with it's capital in Murmánska, was declared by Eret Filpa in February. The first armed clashes between the factions started in February. The civil war ended with the victory of the reds, who took Roudanjärvi on August 1st, and the conflict ended with the conditional surrender of the Esti Free State on the 3rd.

## Background

Following [[great-war]], tensions rose in the Karjan Confederation, with anti-monarchist protests accross several cities accross Routanmaa and Helkanmaa. In 1919, around 300 workers were executed by the Royal Guard in Arkankeli, leading to further protests. King Kristian IV, the final king of Karjania, fell ill in November of 1920, and due to his advanced age, would never recover. During his last public appearance, the new year's speech on December 31st, 1919, he used a wheelchair,and was blind in his left eye. He died early in the morning of January 8th, in Pietari Royal Hospital (now National Hospital of Karjania). His death was officially announced at 7:48am. It's widely reported he didn't die of natural causes, but rather euthanized by his doctor after several hours of suffering. A national day of mourning was announced, causing the early closure of factories and shops. Many factory workers gathered in Pietari Central Square (now Red Square), singing songs and celebrating the death of the king. Among them was Yrjö Sinnar, former writer for socialist magazine The Red Lion, who was forced to work in a factory after it's banning by royalty. He gave a speech to his colleagues and fellow workers, over 3 hours long. By noon, over 20,000 people had gathered to listen. The crowd first marched through Pietari into the National Royal Opera House, where they performed several socialist songs and anthems. Then, they marched to the Pietari Hippodrome, where they forced officials to cancel all horse races held that day,and Sinnar once again gave a speech, this time in front of a crowd over 63,000, sat in the grandstand of Pietari Hippodrome. Later, around 8pm, they marched to the Pietari Royal Prison and Court, and demanded the release of several socialist writers, who were imprisoned for criticising nobility. Then, once it was past midnight, they marched to the building of the Council of Nobles, and demanded entry. 12 nobles were still in the building when they arrived. 9 of them were playing cards and drinking in the common room. They were all lined up against the wall and shot. Count Aaltonen was the only one of the remaining 3 who was awake, he escaped through the window after hearing gunfire. When the crowd could not find any more people in the council building, they planted explosives and set fire to it. Around 29 people were killed in the fire, Including nobles and their families. The fire spread through the streets, and by morning had claimed over 1000 casualties. On the morning of the 9th of January, Yrjö Sinnar was officially declared the leader of the Pietari Commune, and declared war on the Karjan Confederation. Heikki Aaltonen was regarded as the interim leader of the Karjan Confederation, he officially disbanded it on the 13th of January, and declared the Karjanian Republic. On the 11th of January, Yrjö Sinnar declared the United Socialist States of Karjania after the borders of the Pietari Commune expanded significantly past the city limits. Both sides claimed ownership over the entirety of the Karjan Confederation. Later in January, in the city of Toksavaara and  surrounding smaller villages,mass protests and marches broke out, in favour of Esti independence, later culminating in the Great Esti Uprising on the 21st of January, led by Rauno Peetre, later leading to the creation of the Esti Fred State, which was recognised as independent by the Karjanian Republic in exchange of the Esti Free State declaring war on the socialists. In the North of Karjania, neither side had made any progress towards conquering it, the land had became uncontrolled and the situation was unsustainable. Seeing this, socialist revolutionary Eret Filpa declared the Saamit Socialist State, with it's capital in Murmánska. Immediately, they officially joined the war on the side of the USSK.

## Warfare

### Expansion of the USSK, SSS and the Republic

Before armed clashes between the factions had started, the USSK expanded massively, conquering lands under control of neither side. They had taken Helkanmaa, Arkankeli, Kalevala, Välivesi and the Kajuunian coast, establishing a border with the Saamit Socialist State, an important step to achieving their logistical superiority to the Republicans. Most of the civilian population in these lands supported the socialist cause, and conquests were without force or casualties.  The Saamit Socialist State took southern lands all the way to Åulot, and by the start of the armed conflict, less than 30% of the population within the land of the Saamit Socialist State identified as Saamit or spoke the Saamit language. They had also claimed ownership of the [[cuohpajalla]], but the archipelago stayed de facto neutral during the conflict, although they were sent supplies by both the USSK and SSS. The Karjanian Republic started expanding it's territory late compared to the socialists, and was forced to take plenty of socialist leaning settlements by force, which weakened it's army before warfare between the factions had started.

### Winter Campaign

On February 27th, the socialists began the winter campaign, which lasted for approximately a month. Originally, their plan was to conquer the land leading to the Skjarnish border between Lake Lägrebothnia and Övrebothnia and cut the Republic in half, which would've meant sure victory. However, despite their technological and numerical superiority, the Republican troops halted their advances at the battle of Surtuja, and later began marching into the claims of the USSK, conquering several significant settlements. For around 2 weeks, the frontlines were stuck in place, with no advances from either side. Then, on March 23rd, Saamit infantry and cavalry began their invasion of the Republic in large numbers, causing the Republicans to have to fight on two separate fronts. The city of Åulot fell on the 25th without much resistance, and by the beginning of April, the socialists had taken around 35% of the original territory of the Republicans. During this period, [[rudania]] had began sending equipment and volunteers to the socialists, as well as supplies and horses.  By the time the Esti Free State had raised it's army and attempted to cross the border into the USSK, they were outnumbered significantly, the defending force made up of largely Rudanians. The battle of Narovsky, on the shore of Lake Inkeri, was the deadliest clash for the Esti Free State during the war, with over 17,000 of it's troops dead or injured. However, the USSK chose not to attempt taking Toksavaara, believing that although it would've meant the sure surrender of the Esti Free State,it was more worthwhile for the socialist to focus their attention on the Karjanian Repubic.

### Usumi, Esti and Inkerian Campaigns

The Usumi campaign, also known as operation Tsirvakka was a plan developed by the socialists that they believed would result in the surrender of the Republicans by May. It relied on being able to conquer the entire Usumi region, which would've meant encircling Roudanjärvi and splitting the Republic in two halves. At first, the socialists were successful, atfter the battle of Syväsjärvi, they were only kilometers from the city of Tsirvakka, which was the largest city outside Roudanjärvi within the Republic. The battle of Tsirvakka lasted almost a month and was by far the deadliest clash of the civil war. At first, the Republicans succeeded in defending the city, keeping the socialists from reaching inside Tsirvakka for 13 days between April 13th and 26th. During this period, the roads surrounding Tsirvakka from the north were littered with corpses of soldiers, civilians and animals. Over 35,000 people,had lost their lives in this period known as "The two weeks of death". On April 27th, the socialists broke through the defences of the Republic and entered the city. They executed everyone who didn't swear allegiance to the socialist revolutionary cause, while the city was split in half between the two factions who continued their clashes on the streets of Tsirvakka. On May 9th, a large Rudanian reinforcement arrived to help the socialists, who, after one final charge, took control of the city of Tsirvakka. The city was lit up in flames by the escaping Republican army, causing more casualties for both troops and civilians. Both hospitals in the city had burnt down, which meant injured and sick people would have to be treated at home of by the army medics of the socialists. The Republicans devised a "last hurrah", the Esti and Inkerian campaigns. Their plan was to invade and conquer the Inkeria region through the borders of the Esti Free State, and then attempt taking the city of Pietari. At first, they were successful, their quick and agile army taking Narovsky and setting up camp only 17 kilometers from Pietari after just one day, surprising the socialists. However, as the USSK became aware of this, they quickly requested more troops from Rudania, who obliged, and despite reaching the edges of the city, the Republicans were unsuccessful in their attempt to take Pietari. Their troops fled, and the Republic put all it's focus on defending Roudanjärvi. Hollosendian troops, supplies and equipment arrived to the Republicans during this period of time.

### Summer and the battle of Roudanjärvi

On the 19th of May, the USSK began advancing forth again, with the aim of taking Roudanjärvi and officially ending the war, uniting Karjania into one. By the 7th of July, they took Narovsky from the Esti Free State, and the Karjanian Republic was reduced to just the city of Roudanjärvi. First, the socialists attempted to negotiate a surrender with the Republicans, but their attempts were unsuccessful. After a month long siege, the socialists started their attempts to enter the city by force on the 30th of July. The untrained, malnourished, outnumbered Republican troops were unsuccessful in their attempt to keep the city, and on the morning of August 1st, Roudanjärvi, along with the entirety of the Karjanian Republic, fell into the hands of socialists. The Esti Free State, not wanting more unnecessary bloodshed between the sides, surrendered on August 3rd, without any socialist troops in it's borders. Yrjö Sinnar delivered a victory speech on the 4th of August to almost 300,000 people in Pietari.

### Treaty of Tsirvakka

The Treaty of Tsirvakka was signed by the leaders of the USSK, SSS, Republic and the Esti Free State on August 17th, in the burnt down city council building of Tsirvakka. This location was chosen by the socialists for propaganda purposes, as the destruction of it was caused by the Republicans. The treaty included the merging of the Esti Free State into the United Socialist States of Karjania, among others. While Rauno Peetre was required to, and he sighed the treaty, Aaltonen was only there for propaganda purposes. He had been caught and imprisoned when Roudanjärvi fell, and was kept in the Roudanjärvi royal prison since. Two days after, the Saamit Socialist State officially signed the Union Agreement in Murmánska, merging the state into the United Socialist States of Karjania. The local government of Kulanmaan officially recognised the USSK on the 27th of August.

## Aftermath

Approximately 2,000 generals and government officials from the Republic were executed in the next seven months. Heikki Aaltonen was shot by firing squad in Roudanjärvi on the 30th of November, 1920. Rauno Peetre, the leader of the Esti Fred State, was given a small house on the outskirts of Perona, where he lived until his death in a tree felling accident on December 23rd, 1927. Eret Filpa was chosen as the governor of the Saamit State within the USSK, while Yrjö Sinnar became the leader. By 1921, governments across [[avium]] had accepted the USSK as the one and only government of Karjania. Later, Karjania developed good relations with other socialist nations. The Karjanian economy overwent several significant changes, a large number of factories being built all accross the land of a thousand lakes. The socialist government began financing sciences and sports. Most people had accepted the USSK, as living standards had improved compared to the oppression by the royals, and the quality of education had become significantly superior, although it contained large amounts of state propaganda.
