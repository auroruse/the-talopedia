The Karjanmaa-Ajo is a G1 harness race held at Åulot Racecourse in Karjania yearly, on the last Sunday of May. It is one of the most prestigious races in [[evria]], with a purse over $1,000,000. It has been held every year since 1900, with the exception of 4 years due to [[great-war]]. It is held over 1600 meters on a dirt surface, with 12 horses competing.

## History

In 1897, with the growing popularity of the Åulot Prix, members of the Karjanian Horse Breeding Association (KHY) and the Karjanian Horse Racing Federation (KHRF), as well as noblemen from Åulot and the surrounding North Routanmaa region decided to create the Karjanmaa-Ajo, which quickly became known as the most prestigious harness race in the country. For the first event in 1900, over 170 horses sought entry, but the race can only be contested by 12. Among the final grid were horses from several nations, but the inaugural event would be won by legendary Karjanian racehorse Aarteeni, who would go on to win in 1901 as well, the final race he would ever go on to start. The 1902 and 1903 editions were won by Pepel and Luna respectively, two horses from [[rudania]]. The 1904 race was won by the Livonian Užtemimas. The next 3 were all won by Karjanian horses, while the 1908 event would be won by the Skjarnish Solsken. In 1909, Hiljaisuus Olova became the first mare to win the race.  Solsken became the second ever horse to win twice with his victory in 1911. Due to the destruction of Åulot Racecourse by fires in 1912, the 1913 edition was especially prestigious and had record viewing numbers, with the King of Karjania in attendance. The race was won by the Karjanian stallion Raidat. Between 1915-1918, the Karjanmaa-Ajo wasn't held due to the great war. The first event after the war in 1919 brought previously unseen amounts of international competition, with the race won by the Ymirian Schwarzwald. Åulot Racecourse continued hosting events during the Karjanian Civil War, but the Karjanmaa-Ajo was cancelled for 1920. The 1923 edition, won by Haru No Sakura of [[nichirin]] was the first Karjanmaa-Ajo won by a horse outside Evria. In 1930, Kevätkukka set the fastest race winning time. The 1931 edition, won by Fuego Veloz from [[auritania]] was the first race won by an Elysian horse. The 1934 event was won by Lólány from Turulia, the second mare to win the Karjanmaa-Ajo, while the next edition in 1935 was won by the mare Iluzja from [[cortesia]].
