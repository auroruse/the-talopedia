**Jasmine Sarah Petrova** (born February 13th, 1889) is an Elysian economic theorist and politician serving as the current Minister of Finance for the [[elysian-state-of-unity|Elysian State of Unity]] under Supreme Protector [[archer-maine]] and [[arianne-malloran]].

Born and raised in New Winscor, her family was deeply entrenched within New Winscor's (today's Foundry) business scene. Her mother was a clerk for the Wonderhill Mining Corporation, and her father was a senior board member of the same company. Her affluent upbringing meant she could enter the University of Ganshowe as a Finance major. During her studies, she began developing ideas for a functional model for a combined public/private economy for Elysia, the end goal being to facilitate fairer working conditions while still maintaining a growing economy. She would try for office multiple times; however, she would gain very little overall support, ending up working as a clerk in Metro Regima until the Civil War, when her ideas were noticed and praised by the up-and-coming Archer Maine, who would later appoint her as his government's Finance Minister. She is characterized as being "as tough as iron" by her colleagues, maintaining the same cadence she held dear during the Reconstruction Period of nigh-austerity, only kept at bay by Politburo majority votes.
