**Alyssa Daphne Traton** (born January 23rd, 1894) is an Elysian politician, writer, and political theorist. She is the first chairwoman of the Gestaltist Party of Elysia and a prominent member of the Politburo. Traton majored in Brantish at the University of Dogma, frequenting leftist philosophical circles for some time prior to her start in writing as a columnist for the city's major newspaper, the Corona Star. She went into hiding for some time during the Years of Oppression, bouncing from hiding place to hiding place, before ultimately making contact with Archer Maine's Spartacus Brigade. She would be assigned as the propaganda department's lead writer, wherein her fiery way of writing would attract the attention of Archer Maine, with whom she would develop the ideology of Gestaltism following the end of the Civil War.

A founding member of the Gestaltist Party and one of the two minds behind the ideology's development, Alyssa maintains an important position in Elysian politics. She occasionally gives lectures regarding Gestaltist philosophy at both domestic and foreign universities. She maintains an affair with Supreme Protector Maine, the fact of which remains an open secret within the Politburo.
