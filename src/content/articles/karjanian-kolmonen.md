The Karjanian Kolmonen (KK) (Routan: *Karjanmaan Kolmonen*)**is a professional football league in **[[karjania]], the third and bottom tier of the Karjanian football pyramid, below the [[karjanian-secondary-league]] and the [[karjanian-premier-league]]. It was founded in 1921 following the Karjanian Civil War. The winner is promoted to the KSL. There are 16 teams competing for the title. Teams competing in the KK also play in the Karjanian Cup.

## Structure

The Karjanian Kolmonen has 16 teams, which means a total of 30 Matchdays. Every team plays eachother twice, all teams playing 15 games at home and 15 games away. Teams are awarded 3 points for a win, 1 for a draw and 0 for a loss. The teams also compete in the Karjanian Cup, where they play from the first round onwards. The winner of the Kolmonen is promoted to the Karjanian Secondary League, and there is no relegation from the Kolmonen. Seasons have been held every year since 1921 without exceptions.

## History

The Karjanian Kolmonen traces it's origins back to the reorganization of Karjanian football following the end of the Karjanian Civil War. In 1921, the Karjanian Football Association (KFA) founded the Karjanian Kolmonen, originally with 7 teams and 12 Matchdays. This format was kept until 1931. The inaugural season was won by Leijonat Arkankeli with 24 points. The 1922 season was won by Jieknaluokta Polar Bears from [[cuohpajalla|Cuohpajálla]].  1830 Pietari won in 1923 and Leijonat Arkankeli took their second title in 1924, with a last game victory over rivals Narovsky 1898,who went on to win in 1925. Tolvajärvi PS won in 1926, promoting them to the KSL for the first time. Rautaniemi JK took the 1927 title with the record for most points under the 12 game format, earning 26.  Romsa JK took the 1928 title with 19 points, a record low, winning on goal difference over Kajuunia Pines, who got promoted after winning the 1929 title. The 1930 season, the last under the 12 game format was won by Narovsky JK. For the 1931 season, a new, 8 team and 14 game format was introduced. The season was won by Leijonat Arkankeli, their third title, making them the record holders. From the 1932 season onwards, the current format was introduced, and the title was won by Okrug JK. Romsa JK became the second team ever to win multiple titles after winning the 1933 season, their second championship.
