The Thousand Lakes Derby is a G1 horse race in [[karjania]], held on the first Saturday of October every year since 1920 at the [[arkankeli-racecourse]]. It is a part of the Karjanian Thoroughbred Triple Crown, and is one of 4 G1 rated flat faces in Karjania. It is run over 2400 meters on a dirt surface at the Arkankeli Racecourse oval. The purse, at $500,000 is inside the top 10 in Karjania for any type of horse racing event. The Thousand Lakes Derby is attended by horses from all over [[evria]]. Horse above 2 years old can compete, both genders. It has been held every year since 1920 without interruption.

## History

The Thousand Lakes Derby was first held in 1920, during the Karjanian Civil War. The first 2 editions in 1920 and 1921 were both won by the Karjanian Vahvat Jalat. In 1922, the Livonian stallion Vèjo Spyris became the first international winner, after a 7 length victory. Unfortunately, he would not compete in Karjania again after his crew were denied entry into the 1923 edition, which became the only running of the Thousand Lakes Derby where not a single Karjanian horse placed in the top 3. The race was won by the Elvesterian Tumbling Winds, the first mare to achieve victory in the competition. Hopeinen Uni won the 1924 Thousand Lakes Derby, becoming the second Karjanian horse to do so. The mare Kasztanka from [[cortesia]] won the next edition in 1925, becoming the first Cortesian horse to win a G1 race in Karjania. The 1926 and 1927 runnings were won by the Arvernois Sommeil d'or and the Ymirian Bezaubernder Blick respectively. The legendary Karjanian stallion Onnellinen won 3 Thousand Lakes Derbies in a row between 1928 and 1930. He is widely considered the greatest Karjanian Thoroughbred of all time.  In 1931, Ezüstpata became the first Turulian horse to win the Thousand Lakes Derby. Erityisviikko, who won in 1932, became the first Karjanian mare to win. Jalallinen Hylje won 2 in a row, in 1933 and 1934, making him the third horse to win multiple Thousand Lakes Derbies after Vahvat Jalat and Onnellinen. Rostam Cyrus, a Varahmehri stallion won the 1935 edition by 14 lengths, the largest winning distance in the history of the race.
