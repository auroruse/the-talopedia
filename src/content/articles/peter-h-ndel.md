**Peter Händel **(born April 17th, 1881) is an Elysian-Nordmarker doctor of biochemistry and government official serving as the current Minister of Health for the Elysian State of Unity under Supreme Protector [Archer Maine](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1Fe6bcre-u3qbes5A2N92ol9Rz89aOKpE9SHN1n5Ae5Y/edit). He completed his doctoral thesis at the University of Hammersburg before moving to Elysia due to complications arising from the Holy Evrian Civil War. He found great success working for the former pharmaceutical company Lyra before being arrested for funneling personal and company funds to the Revolutionary Guard early on during the Elysian Civil War. He was sent to the Rivet Maximum Security Correctional Facility, where he would lose the use of his left leg as a consequence of the staff’s treatment of him. He often appears in public with a cane and brace, but prefers to use a wheelchair otherwise. As Minister of Health, he’s responsible for administering the Unity State’s healthcare infrastructure, disease surveillance, and some aspects of the State’s welfare system.
