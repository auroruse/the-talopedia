This is a list of political parties in [[varahmehr]]. Nine parties hold seats in the Majles-e Nāmāyandagān, the lower chamber of the [[majles-e-melli|Majles-e Mellī]], following the general elections of 5 March 1933, the first held under the party system. All nine were founded in 1932, when the four informal factions that had organized federal politics since the founding of the federation divided along the lines of their internal wings. The government of Vazir-e Azam Ehteram Qaedi is formed by Ensāf (ENS), the Party of the National Workers (KAR), and the Roshanfekrān (RON), and relies on the support of Panāh (PAN). The largest opposition party is the Party of the Federal Compact (PYM), led by former Vazir-e Azam Nozar Shariati.

## Overview

From the signing of the Peymān-e Mellī in 1878 until 1932, federal politics was organized around four factions. They had no membership, program or discipline, and delegates stood for election as individuals. The census of 1929 recorded a population of 52,599,000, and the Reapportionment Act of 1932 enlarged the Majles-e Nāmāyandagān from 57 seats to 391 to match it. Contesting several hundred seats required organized slates of characters, which the factions could not supply, so each divided into parties along the lines of its existing wings.

The Reapportionment Act also shifted the chamber's weight toward Ahanestan and Dashtestan. The heirs of the Mardom-e Kār rose from under a third of the chamber to 195 of 391 seats, one short of a majority. The heirs of the Peymān-dārān, which had held the chancellorship since 1924, fell to 73.

## Electoral system

Delegates to the Majles-e Nāmāyandagān are elected by single transferable vote in 82 multi-member constituencies of three to seven seats, drawn within shahrestān boundaries.

Elections to single offices - Ostāndār, Shahrdār, and the seats of the Shurā-ye
Āyan - use a ranked ballot with one winner. These ballots include the option *None of the Above* (هیچ‌کدام, *Hich-kodām*), which, if it prevails, disqualifies every candidate and requires a fresh election within sixty days.

The Vazir-e Azam is elected by the Majles-e Nāmāyandagān after each general election by the same single-winner method. Al-Sahrā, a *ham-peymān* territory, is not yet represented in the Majles; the terms of its representation are under negotiation.

| Party | Position |
| --- | --- |
| ENS | Engagement conditional on rights; no agreement that compromises Article Three. |
| PYM | Cautious internationalism within constitutional limits; defends the 1930 deployment to Shivon as lawful. |
| KAR | Trade agreements and concessions only with enforceable labor clauses. |
| RON | Open exchange in education, scholarship and the press. |
| PAN | Humanitarian internationalism; opposes security commitments to authoritarian states. |
