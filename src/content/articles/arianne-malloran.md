**Arianne Ellen Malloran** (born March 19th, 1896) is an [Elysian](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1KTeXRWuQuhHpeNn6SUDF9j-A1W1KeLUZ4A7bb4N0CGw/edit) politician and military officer who served as a decorated officer during the civil war, the General-of-the-Army and Minister of Defense under Supreme Protector Archer Maine, as well as the Unity State's second Supreme Protector.
