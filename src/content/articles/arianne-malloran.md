**Arianne Ellen Malloran** (born March 19th, 1896) is an [Elysian](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1KTeXRWuQuhHpeNn6SUDF9j-A1W1KeLUZ4A7bb4N0CGw/edit) politician and military officer serving as the Minister of Defense for the Unity State under Supreme Protector [Archer Maine](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1Fe6bcre-u3qbes5A2N92ol9Rz89aOKpE9SHN1n5Ae5Y/edit). She also serves as the current General of the Army for the Grand Army of Unity. She is responsible for organizing and coordinating not only Elysia’s ground forces, but every other branch of the military. She is notable for being not only the first woman in Elysia to attain such a rank, but also the youngest, having been promoted to her position when she was only 31 years old. Her rapid rise through the ranks is mostly owed to her streak of victories she achieved during the Elysian Civil War. She stands as the most highly decorated officer in the service of the Unity State.
