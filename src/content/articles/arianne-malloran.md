**Arianne Ellen Malloran** (born March 19th, 1896) is an [[elysian-state-of-unity|Elysian]] politician and military officer who served as a decorated officer during the civil war, the General-of-the-Army and Minister of Defense under Supreme Protector [[archer-maine]], as well as the Unity State's second Supreme Protector.

Originally a chemistry teacher in her home city of [[new-winscor]], Malloran's life was thoroughly uprooted by President Vincent Stone's policies during the [[years-of-oppression]], landing her and her partner in government-built labour camps purely because of their sexual orientation. In the wake of the Spartacus Division's campaign of liberation across the Elysian countryside, Malloran would lead a daring prison riot against her camp's guards, an action that would gain the attention of the Division's commanders, enrolling her into leadership training and assigning her the command of an entire platoon. Throughout [[elysian-civil-war|the Civil War]], she would prove to be a capable officer, rising to the rank of Major before the end of the Civil War, later gaining the promotion to the rank of General-of-the-Army and appointment to the role of Defense Minister during the Reconstruction Period.

As Defense Minister, she would oversee a slew of reforms that reorganized the Unity State's disorganized post-civil war guard units into a fully functioning and formidable army, capable of standing toe-to-toe with the larger regional powers around the Unity State. Her various decorations and fame obtained during the civil war, as well as her promotion to the rank of General-of-the-Army at the age of 31, and being the first-ever woman to obtain such a rank, made her a well-known figure to the public, a fact which would ultimately be cemented when she was elected as Supreme Protector in 1934. She remains the most highly decorated officer in the history of the Unity State.

## Early Life

Arianne Ellen Malloran was born on the 19th of March, 1896 in New Winscor, U.S.E. the daughter of pharmacist Michael Malloran and locally renowned hatter Mary Newmaker. Her father was often absent from home due to the nature of his work and combined with his involvement in local politics, making her much closer with her mother when she did have someone at home with her.

However, something she would pick up from his father was an interest in chemistry. During the fewtimes when she could spend time with his father, he would entertain her by showing off various chemical reactions in his ad-hoc home laboratory, as well as assisting her in recreating the reactions herself. This would later manifest in school, with her excelling in chemistry, eventually landing her to study the subject in the University of Ganshowe with a scholarship. She would graduate with a bachelors in chemistry and pedagogy, paving her way to become a teacher of chemistry.

## Teaching Career and Imprisonment

Graduating at the age of 19, she would land a teaching job at New Winscor's Caronen Gymnasium. Around this time, she would reconnect with Ellen Yeong, a fellow teacher at the Gymnasium, and a fast friend of Arianne's. The two'd known each other since elementary school, but would properly reconnect in Arianne's later years in University. The two would quickly fall for each other and officially begin dating in 1919, moving together into an apartment in New Winscor.

Just as her career had begun, she would be hit by a major tragedy. The recently elected President Vincent Stone had begun cracking down on all forms of political opposition to his clique within the government. While originally targeting primarily communist and socialist politicians, his unopposed policies would begin cracking down on every people group he saw as "undesireable". In February of 1920, she and Ellen would be arrested and separated, before promptly being sent to a government run labour camp, specifically Camp Hotel.

Built specifically to house members of sexual minorities, the conditions inside Camp Hotel were grueling to Malloran. Guards and personnel constantly harrassed, exploited and assaulted prisoners, while subjecting them to grueling physical labour. Malloran would do her best to stay under the radar, however would eventually fall victim to one of the camp's guards. The ensuing assault would leave her with a large and permanent scar across her face, running from her left cheek to across her nose.

Malloran would wither away in the camp for a total of 10 months, losing 15kg of her body weight in the progress, leaving her nearly skeletal by December of 1920. During that month however, her and many other camp prisoners would hear rumours and rumblings of the advancing Spartacus Division. Noting the thinning amount of guards and a nervous atmosphere with the remaining staff, she would begin organizing a plan for an all out revolt in the camp. When the sounds of combat neared the camp, the prisoners seized the opportunity and began disarming guard personnel with what few tools they had. Overall, her actions would succeed in minimizing friendly casualties, eventually linking up with members of the Spartacus Division.

## Military Career

### Service in the Civil War

Following their joining up with the Spartacus Division, the surviving prisoners from Camp Hotel would begin spreading word up the chain of command regarding Malloran's participation in the revolt. Impressed by her talent in leadership, Archer Maine would quickly forward her into leadership training, where she would spend some time under Captain Marc Cornelius, a retired Rumaic-Elysian Ranger Captain who brought her under his wing, taught the basics of military strategy and tactics, and worked alongside her for the duration of the civil war.

Following the end of her training period, she was promoted to Ensign in January of 1921, and given command of her own platoon on the Northwestern front. Her skill in leadership would quickly begin to show, with her racking up a plethora of victories against White Army formations in and around Skandario. After her promotion to 1st Lieutenant, she would oversee the conquest of Charlotte, bringing a major city into the control of the Revolutionary Guard. She would continue fighting in Northwestern Elysia for the entire duration of the Battle of the Tango-Line, managing to create the most advances out of any Guard field commander prior to Colonel Elwood's defection, and the subsequent collapse of White Army resistance around the Tango-Line. Pursuing the retreating White Army forces, she would eventually find herself participating in the Battle of Nexus, advancing on the city from the North.

Following the decisive defeat of the White Army at Nexus, Malloran would be promoted to Major. Her contirbutions to the war effort had her assigned with the task of hunting down any remaining White Army holdout formations still holed up in the South and North of the nation. By June of 1922, all White Army remnants had moved onto partisan warfare, and the matter was transferred to the care of the Civil Guard.

## Political Career

### Minister of Defense

Following the official formation of the Unity State, Maine quickly recognized Malloran for her skill as a military officer, appointing her into the position of Minister of Defense as part of the first cabinet of the Politburo. While an important voice in the crowd, her concerns and campaigning for widespread military reform were often ignored during the early years of the Unity State, only able to implement rudamentary changes. Her programs were largely ignored due to the Politburo's focus on reconstruction.

Only in 1927 would her reforms finally go through. Through her initiative, the Civil Guard would be integrated into the Ministry of the Interior, and a comprehensive armed forces would take the Revolutionary Guard's place, in the form of the [[grand-army-of-unity]].

As recognition for her service to the Unity State, Maine would promote Malloran to the position of General-of-the-Army, making her both the Minister of Defense, as well as the head of the Army. She would hold these two offices concurrently along with her position as commander of SAHARACOM.

### Operation KNIGHTFALL

On the 22nd of June, 1931, Malloran was called to accompany both George Elwood and Supreme Protector Archer Maine to Shinkeisei over intel regarding the exact location of MAJESTY's headquarters in Rudania. She would remain in Shinkeisei for some time afterwards, organizing the Grand Army's cooperation with the Shogunate's Kagemusha. Following the succesful reconnaissance run to scout out the base, she would act as one fo the key strategists for the operation alongside Admiral Suiren Makinami.

On June 6th, 1932, Malloran would accompany the strike force's main force aboard the Aircraft Carrier Kestrel, coordinating the naval and later ground force elements following the spearhead's succesful infiltration and capture of Conway.

## As Supreme Protector

Following Archer Maine's announcement of retirement, as well as the upcoming election for the next Supreme Protector, Malloran would quickly announce her plans to run for office. Her campaign largely focused on maintaining the already strong relations Elysia had forged with the rest of the world, while also taking a firmer stance against hostile regimes and authoritarianism. Her status as a well known war hero assisted her in her campaign greatly, eventually winning over Alyssa Traton by a slim margin. She would be inagurated into the office on the 22nd of December, 1934.

## Personal Life

Following her inaguration as Supreme Protector, Malloran officially lives in the Protectorate Palace in Nexus. Her decor in her office hasn't changed from how Maine previously kept it, with the main change being her keeping a memento of her former partner in the form of her golden dragonfly earring on display in her office.

Malloran is an active fan of music, especially of the famous Elysian duo Blue Moon. She's developed a mild but professional friendship with many members of the Politburo, particularly General George Elwood.
