**Arianne Ellen Malloran** (born March 19th, 1896) is an [Elysian](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1KTeXRWuQuhHpeNn6SUDF9j-A1W1KeLUZ4A7bb4N0CGw/edit) politician and military officer who served as a decorated officer during the civil war, the General-of-the-Army and Minister of Defense under Supreme Protector Archer Maine, as well as the Unity State's second Supreme Protector.

Originally a chemistry teacher in her home city of New Winscor, Malloran's life was thoroughly uprooted by President Vincent Stone's policies during the Years of Oppression, landing her and her partner in government-built labour camps purely because of their sexual orientation. In the wake of the Spartacus Division's campaign of liberation across the Elysian countryside, Malloran would lead a daring prison riot against her camp's guards, an action that would gain the attention of the Division's commanders, enrolling her into leadership training and assigning her the command of an entire platoon. Throughout the Civil War, she would prove to be a capable officer, rising to the rank of Major before the end of the Civil War, later gaining the promotion to the rank of General-of-the-Army and appointment to the role of Defense Minister during the Reconstruction Period.

As Defense Minister, she would oversee a slew of reforms that reorganized the Unity State's disorganized post-civil war guard units into a fully functioning and formidable army, capable of standing toe-to-toe with the larger regional powers around the Unity State. Her various decorations and fame obtained during the civil war, as well as her promotion to the rank of General-of-the-Army at the age of 31, and being the first-ever woman to obtain such a rank, made her a well-known figure to the public, a fact which would ultimately be cemented when she was elected as Supreme Protector in 1934. She remains the most highly decorated officer in the history of the Unity State.
